# Playbook: Make a decision well

> The PM job is mostly decisions. This is the default path for non-trivial ones.

## Steps

1. **Phrase the decision** as a question — not a discussion. "Do we ship X by Q3?", not "How are we thinking about X?".
2. **Frame** with `pm-strategist` — pick the lens (RICE, JTBD, 2x2, etc.) and apply it.
3. **Get the data** — `data-analyst` for any number underpinning the call.
4. **Build a decision memo** with `templates/decision-memo.md`. At minimum 2 options, honest trade-offs.
5. **Pre-mortem** — dispatch `critic` to attack the recommendation.
6. **Surface to decision-maker** — wrap with `stakeholder-comms` if the audience is exec.
7. **Capture the call** with `/decision`. Even a "we decided to wait" is a decision.
8. **Communicate the decision** — short note to anyone affected.
9. **Set the revisit trigger** — what would cause us to reopen this? File it in the decision entry.

## Anti-patterns

- Don't make a decision without naming the falsifier (what would change your mind).
- Don't relitigate decided things in casual conversations — point to the decision file.
- Don't decide implicitly. If a path was chosen, write it down.
