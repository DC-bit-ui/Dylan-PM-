## PROMPT 4 — Corrective measures + opportunity pathway
Update **only the action screen** (or add one directly after the profile if it doesn't exist): **"The opportunity pathway — from decline to recovery."** For the declining business, show:
- **Corrective measures** — the management changes (kept generic: grazing / land-management practice changes that rebuild ground cover, soil carbon and productive capacity), each tagged with its effect on the trajectory.
- **The pathway** — a before/after: applying it bends the Ecological Condition Index and the DSCR back up, the rating improves (D · 38 → projected B · 66 over ~4 years), repayment risk falls, and the business begins generating carbon/reef credits (new income). Frame it as turning an at-risk loan into a recovering, revenue-generating, de-risked asset — good for the borrower and the bank, never surveillance/foreclosure.
