# Context Pack — Facts & Framing (for the demo prompt)

**Date:** 2026-07-15 · Reference facts for the concept and the Claude Design prompt. Respect these; they earn credibility.

## Positioning (non-negotiable)
**AgriProve = Australia's leading natural capital analytics service provider** — the measurement and risk-intelligence infrastructure. NOT a "soil-carbon project developer." Carbon/reef credits are one downstream output and revenue line, not the identity, and must not lead.

## The concept focus (non-negotiable)
The product is the **risk profiling of the agricultural businesses in the bank's book** (Hobbs' angle) — which businesses are on a declining trajectory before it hits impairments, and the physical-climate risk the bank must disclose. AgriProve can profile a property **remotely, with no farmer sign-up required.** We are NOT here to bring the bank new farmers/projects — that customer-growth angle is a secondary, later upside, not this concept.

## The persona (one screen, risk leads)
An agricultural lender (archetype: **Rabobank** — agri-only, ~$28bn ag book, runs sustainability-linked ag lending, CEFC-partnered, NZBA signatory). The room: **credit-risk / CRO** (leads — PD, expected loss, watchlist, capital, LVR), **sustainability / compliance** (owns AASB S2 / TNFD disclosure), **agribusiness relationship manager** (owns the borrower and the rate), and ultimately the CEO. They buy de-risking, defensible compliance, and protected relationships — not virtue.

## The wedge + verified regulatory framing (say it this way — don't overclaim)
- **AASB S2** — mandatory for large banks since 1 Jan 2025. **Para 29(c)** requires disclosing the amount/% of assets vulnerable to physical climate risk, audit-credibly at asset level — but it allows "reasonable and supportable information without undue cost or effort." → Honest line: *"credible, audit-grade compliance for an ag book needs asset-level data that doesn't exist at paddock scale — we're that,"* NOT "you're legally required to buy this." Emissions reporting got transition relief, so **physical risk is the open, un-serviced gap.**
- **AASB S2 scenario analysis** — ≥2 scenarios incl. 1.5°C → maps to the projection/resilience view.
- **APRA** — no climate capital-relief rule yet; 2026 consultation, blocked by lack of standardised data. → *"positions you ahead of it,"* NOT "delivers relief."
- **TNFD** — accelerating next wave (733+ adopters, ISSB formalising), NOT yet mandatory in Australia. → "TCFD circa 2017," not "required."

## The high-level outputs the bank receives (show at output level, not the indicators beneath)
Portfolio triage (which businesses/tranches are improving / stable / declining, with rating + direction) · a per-business risk verdict (rating + trajectory + credit implication) · disclosure-ready reporting mapped to the mandates · a recommended action + financing option. (Credits / reef story = later upside, not the demo's focus.)

## Commercial model (context — the demo is diagnosis-led, so this is background)
How the bank engages the service, recommended sequence: **risk-profiling-as-a-service** (per-property subscription, ~$10k/property illustrative, across a nominated tranche) → **capital / ICAAP positioning** (certify lower-risk tranches; ~2–5% performance fee on capital positioned — APRA-dependent) → **risk-priced lending** (rate tied to the verified trajectory). Credit offtake, establishment finance, CEFC blended capital = adjacent/later. **Pilot:** profile ~50 of the bank's own properties across 2–3 ecoregions (~$500k, 12 months) → produces the first Australian data linking natural-capital trajectory to loan performance on their own book.

## Live vs R&D (keep R&D under the hood)
Live: AgriProve's satellite + analytics foundations and multi-year history. R&D (under the hood, later): the specific biological-indicator remote sensing, the probability model, the counterfactual attribution engine. Lead with the deliverable result; don't present R&D as live measurement.
