**Purpose:** everything Fable 5 needs to **write the Claude Design prompt** that produces a working demo of the AgriProve risk-profiling concept.

## The workflow
1. Open **Claude Fable 5**, **effort: xhigh**. Upload all four files in this pack.
2. Paste the brief from **`01-FABLE-BRIEF-write-the-claude-design-prompt.md`**.

## The through-line (so nothing drifts)
AgriProve = **natural capital analytics service provider**. The concept = **risk profiling of the agricultural businesses in the bank's book** — see which are declining before it hits impairments, and answer the physical-climate risk they must disclose. Biological indicators stay **under the hood**. Diagnosis-led; action/financing secondary. Regulatory claims stay honest.
