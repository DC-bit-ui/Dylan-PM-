## THE PERSONA
An agricultural lender (archetype: Rabobank — agri-only, ~$28bn ag book, already runs sustainability-linked ag lending, CEFC-partnered, NZBA signatory). The room includes credit-risk/CRO, sustainability/compliance, agribusiness lending, and ultimately the CEO. They buy customers, de-risking, and defensible compliance — not virtue.

## THE WEDGE (why they engage at all)
Banks are now legally required (AASB S2, mandatory for large banks since Jan 2025) to disclose the physical climate risk inside their lending books — but the measurement infrastructure doesn't exist at scale. AgriProve fills that gap. Lead with the regulatory problem (the stick), then the upside (the carrot). Verified framing to respect: AASB S2 §29(c) requires disclosing the share of assets vulnerable to physical climate risk, audit-credibly at asset level — but it allows "reasonable and supportable information without undue cost or effort," so the honest line is "credible compliance needs asset-level data that doesn't exist — we're that," not "you're legally required to buy this." APRA capital treatment is coming (2026 consultation) but not a relief rule yet — frame as "positions you ahead of it." TNFD is the accelerating next wave, not yet mandatory.

## THE RISK-PROFILING SERVICE TO OPERATIONALISE (this is the concept)
**What it is:** AgriProve profiles the physical-climate / natural-capital risk of agricultural businesses — the bank's existing ag borrowers and its lending pipeline — and delivers continuous, independently-verified risk intelligence and disclosure-ready outputs. Crucially, a property can be **profiled remotely from AgriProve's satellite and analytics foundations, with no farmer sign-up required** — which is exactly the measurement infrastructure the disclosure regime demands. The bank uses it to: meet its physical-climate-risk disclosure obligation; see credit risk years before it hits impairments; and price and manage the book.

**How the bank engages it (present the options and recommend a sequence):**
- **Risk profiling as a service** (measurement-as-a-service) — the bank pays a per-property subscription (~$10k/property, illustrative) to profile a nominated tranche of its ag book → risk ratings, direction-of-travel, disclosure-ready reporting. *The core; fastest to value.*
- **Capital / ICAAP positioning** — AgriProve certifies lower-risk tranches for the bank's ICAAP; performance fee (~2–5%, illustrative) on capital positioned. *Higher upside; APRA-dependent — position, don't promise.*
- **Risk-priced (sustainability-linked) lending** — loan pricing tied to the verified risk trajectory (illustrative: improving trajectory → rate improvement; declining → step-up). Turns the risk profile into a live pricing input.
- Credit offtake, establishment finance and CEFC blended-capital sit as **adjacent revenue / later expansion**, not the lead. The concept is the risk intelligence, not the credit deal.

**The pilot (concrete first step):** profile ~50 of the bank's own properties across 2–3 ecoregions (~$500k order of magnitude, 12 months): baseline → quarterly reviews → month-12 decision to expand/refine/conclude. Produces the first Australian data linking natural-capital trajectory to loan performance on the bank's own book — which prices the full rollout.

**The arc:** prove it on a tranche → scale profiling across the whole ag book → nationally across catchments (reef catchments and the Murray-Darling as the water-quality opportunity scales).

**Secondary upside (keep minor — NOT the focus):** as the relationship matures, AgriProve can also bring newly-enrolled businesses, credit offtake, and a co-branded reef marketing story — customer growth layered on top of the risk service. This is upside, not the concept; don't let it take over the pitch.

## THE HIGH-LEVEL OUTPUTS THE BANK RECEIVES (show these at output level, not the indicators beneath)
- A portfolio view: which properties/tranches are improving, stable, or declining, with a risk rating and direction-of-travel.
- A per-property risk result: an overall rating, trend, and a recommended action + financing option (protect and grow the asset).
- Disclosure-ready reporting mapped to their mandates (AASB S2 §29(c), scenario analysis, ICAAP positioning, TNFD-ready).
- Credits on the balance sheet (first right of refusal) and the co-branded reef story.
- New customers and whole-of-enterprise relationships, nationally.
