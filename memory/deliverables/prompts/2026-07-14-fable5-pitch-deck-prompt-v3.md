## STRATEGY — LOCKED (build everything on this)

Three layers, in this order:
1. **The hook (hearts): the reef.** The Great Barrier Reef is our generation's most visible climate wound — this is what wins the bank's *new retail and youth* customers.
2. **The business prize (book growth): the Murray-Darling Basin + the reef catchments.** These are Australia's two most important agricultural water systems. The commercial expansion for the bank is *new agri lending and new customers across both*, nationally — not one catchment.
3. **The compounding engine: ACWIS.** As Verterra's ACWIS water-quality-credit capability expands, the bank's book grows with it. The prize is for the bank to become **the bank for water-quality credits in Australia.**

The green establishment loan is the **hook** that recruits *new* farmers into projects and wins them as **lifelong customers across all their lending** (home, machinery, operating, succession) — nationally, across reef and MDB catchments.

AgriProve's existing in-region portfolio (a real book of ~47 grazing enterprises we can bring along) is a **credibility proof and a warm start — NOT the centrepiece.** De-weight it. The pitch is forward-looking: new business, new customers, new projects. Drop the "invitation to tender / one winner per catchment / auction" framing; keep competitive urgency as *first-mover category leadership* ("be the bank that owns this, before a rival does").

# ROLE
You are a world-class pitch strategist and brand storyteller who builds decks that make busy executives lean in. You write with clarity and emotional punch, and you design so a viewer understands each slide in under five seconds — no deciphering. You never bury the lead. Bold, beautiful, simple slides.

# THE JOB
Rebuild AgriProve's pitch deck for bringing a major bank into soil-carbon + water-quality (reef and Murray-Darling) projects. Audience: first AgriProve's founder/MD (to approve), then the bank. The current version is aesthetically good but cluttered, mis-named, and it over-weights our existing book while framing the deal as a one-off tender. Fix all of that. One idea per slide, premium look, every slide pulls the reader forward.

# 1. NAME THE INITIATIVE (kill "Compact")
"The Reef Catchment Compact" is opaque, and now that the scope is reef **and** the Murray-Darling, a reef-only name undersells the business prize. Propose **3–5 names** and recommend one, in two layers:
- **Program name** (AgriProve × bank partnership) — spans clean water from farm to reef and river, nationally, and positions the bank as *the bank for water-quality credits*. Directions: *Source to Sea*, *The Water Standard*, *Clean Country*, *Watershed*.
- **Consumer-facing line the bank uses** — reef-led and emotional: e.g. *"Where you bank helps save the reef."*
Give me something better than these if you can. Do not use "Compact" anywhere.

# 2. THE ONE BIG IDEA (the emotional spine — lead here)
The Great Barrier Reef is **the most visible, most felt indicator of climate change of our generation** — globally, and above all for young Australians who have watched a natural wonder of the world bleach and die in real time. Climate change is abstract; the reef is concrete, personal, heartbreaking. This deck makes a bank the institution that visibly, credibly acts to protect it — and, quietly underneath, builds a national water-quality lending franchise. Open on the reef. Make them feel it before you show them the money.

# 3. THE SYSTEM SLIDE (the centrepiece — make it unmissable and dead simple)
One beautiful, legible slide showing the whole model as a compounding flow (improve on the attached diagram). The story, in sequence:

1. **AgriProve brings the expertise and market position to establish projects** — the hard part no one else can do at scale — and brings a real starter portfolio in-region (mention briefly; do not dwell).
2. **The bank provides green establishment loans.** This is the hook: it funds getting projects off the ground, and it wins the bank a **lifelong customer across ALL their lending**, not a one-off product. Cheap acquisition, huge lifetime value.
3. **New landholders join and start projects** — sequestering carbon and cutting sediment/nutrient runoff to the reef and rivers.
4. **The bank buys the water-quality / reef credits (optional)** and can then claim, truthfully, that it is *the bank directly protecting our national icon* — buying the measured outcome, not sponsoring it.
5. **The engine scales across catchments** — the reef catchments **and the Murray-Darling Basin**, Australia's two great agricultural water systems — recruiting new farmers and new lending nationally.
6. **As ACWIS water-quality credits expand, the bank's book compounds** — it becomes *the bank for water-quality credits in Australia*.
7. **The bank becomes the face of conscious banking for young Australians** — the brand they choose and keep for life.

Present as a single glanceable visual: the reef as the shared prize, a clear compounding loop, minimal words per node. Understandable in five seconds.

# 4. DECK STRUCTURE (tight — one idea per slide, ~8 slides)
1. **Cover** — the new name + the reef line. One arresting reef image/mood. No "tender / confidential / round" jargon.
2. **The reef: our generation's climate story** — the emotional hook (idea #2). Bleaching reality; why the young care.
3. **The insight** — institutions act on risk, return and story, not carbon. (Ruminati/CBA as proof a bank moves when it's in their interest.)
4. **The system** — the compounding flow (idea #3). The centrepiece. Reef hook → MDB + reef growth → ACWIS compounding.
5. **What the bank gets** — the hierarchy of wins, big and simple: lifelong customers across all lending · a de-risked ag book · water-quality/reef credits it can claim · the youth/conscious-banking brand · national scale across reef + Murray-Darling. (Keep the "four budget owners, passing means all four watch a rival win" idea — it works.)
6. **The offer** — green establishment loan ⇄ first-refusal credit rights (ratcheted to market, never fixed); farmer gets a lower rate; "teeth" if commitments lapse. Numbers indicative. Note our existing book as a warm start, lightly.
7. **Why only AgriProve** — market position, landholder relationships + field recruitment engine, carbon + reef credits stacked on one property (co-authored GLM reef method), ACWIS/DROVER water-quality capability, project-establishment know-how. The greenwashing-proof point: every claim backed by property-level telemetry and issued credits — the only reef story that survives ASIC scrutiny.
8. **The ask / next step** — first-mover framing ("be the bank that owns this before a rival does"), and the concrete next step for the bank and for Matthew.

# 5. DESIGN & CLARITY STANDARD (the core fix)
- **One idea per slide.** If it needs deciphering, it fails. ~5-second comprehension.
- **Show, don't cram.** Big type, generous whitespace, few words. Detail → speaker notes, not the slide. (The current slides 2, 3, 4, 7 are too dense; simplify hard.)
- **Premium and aesthetic** — keep the current palette (deep ocean teal, ivory serif, one warm accent); it's good. Consistent type scale. Flat, clean, confident.
- **Every slide's headline states the takeaway,** not a label. "A green loan wins a customer for life," not "Loan structure." (Drop the "Act One/Two…" labels if they add nothing.)
- **The reef is the through-line** — opens, centres the system, closes.
- ~8 slides. Ruthlessly cut.

# 6. GUARDRAILS
- This leverages what AgriProve already has — not a new product build.
- **De-weight the existing book.** The ~47-enterprise portfolio is a real warm start and credibility proof, not the hero. Lead with *new* business and recruitment.
- All financial figures indicative; label them. Don't overclaim the (in-development) attribution/resilience modelling — the pitch stands on credits + story.
- No em dashes or obvious AI tells in the copy. Confident, plain, human.
