## Purpose
Explore how to make HORIZON/AgriProve's assets **bank- and insurer-facing** — designing an offer + story that gets financial institutions to actively participate in soil-carbon + reef-credit projects, reframed around **risk, return, and reputation** rather than carbon.

## Core thesis [high]
Institutions don't act on carbon; they act on risk. The one Australian AgTech institutional-money success (Ruminati) only worked once CBA had its own scope-3 problem to solve. Farmer research confirms the demand side: farmers value **a lower interest rate on their loans** far more than a produce premium. Wedge = reframe carbon + reef credits as a risk/return/reputation instrument for banks and cheaper capital for farmers.

## Emerging offer architecture (illustrative, not fixed)
- Farmer signs to a carbon/reef project → bank fast-tracks a **discounted (green) loan** (a few basis points off).
- Bank takes a **credit share** (illustrative: farmer majority / bank slice / AgriProve slice) and optionally an **offtake right** (buy credits ~15% below spot with a floor) so it can *claim* the reef outcome, not just fund it.
- **Basis-point ratchet** as the stick: rate drifts back toward standard if the farmer misses project commitments (but can't punish for credits not produced — that's already the farmer's downside).
- Bank wins on 5 axes: de-risked ag book, monetisable credits, marketing story, new young/lifelong customers, competitive + regulatory edge.
