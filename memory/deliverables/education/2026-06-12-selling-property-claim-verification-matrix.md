## What was corrected during verification
- The original "exit" framing ("relinquish credits against what was sequestered, keep the rest") was **too soft**. AgriProve's contract (14.4) and FAQ require returning an equivalent of ALL issued ACCUs on landholder-triggered revocation. Fixed.
- Added the agricultural-use commitment as the core honest message (was underplayed).
- Removed the inaccurate "credits registered to you, independent of AgriProve" line; AgriProve is the proponent, with a landholder right to replace it. Fixed.
- Subdivision FAQ corrected: project land must stay agricultural; keep developable blocks out.
