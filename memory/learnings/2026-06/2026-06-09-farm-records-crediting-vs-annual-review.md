## The fact

Farm management records are handled two different ways depending on the job:

- **Annual reviews** — updated as **separate files per annual review** (one file set per review cycle).
- **Crediting rounds** — an **aggregate of the whole period up to crediting**, and this aggregate **forms part of the abatement calculation**.

So they are NOT the same artifact surfaced twice — annual-review records are discrete per-review files; the crediting record is a period-aggregate that feeds abatement.
