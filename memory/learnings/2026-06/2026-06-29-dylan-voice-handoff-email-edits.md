## What changed from my draft → his sent version

- **Channel reality:** it went out as an **email with Cc (Cadel, Athul)**, not a Teams channel post. For a dev handoff, default to email + Cc the relevant leads/devs (Cadel, Athul) rather than a lone channel post.
- **Greeting:** "Hi Gayathri," — measured, not "Hey … excited to get this rolling". **Drop enthusiasm preamble.**
- **Opener = callback:** "As mentioned in our kickoff - please see the first phase of the Prospective Projects Restructure - Project Hub & Dashboard". Lead with the callback + the thing.
- **No emoji.** I used 📎; he cut it. Plain "Attached:".
- **Tentative framing on claims:** he softened "it's a lift, not a rebuild" → "so I believe it is a lift, not a rebuild - let's see!". Hedge confident assertions; invite the dev to test them.
- **Hyphens, not em dashes:** he uses " - " (spaced hyphen) throughout. (Reinforces [[feedback_no_ai_tells]].)
- **Dropped the per-item engineering caveat:** I wrote "(we'll need Athul on the payload)"; he cut it and **Cc'd Athul instead**. Keep the flagged items clean in the body; handle dependencies via Cc / direct, not inline caveats.
- **Reference attachments explicitly:** "The visual walkthrough from kickoff is inside too, under `docs/` + it is attached here."
