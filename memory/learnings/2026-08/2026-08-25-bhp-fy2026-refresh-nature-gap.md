# BHP FY2026 refresh — the nature gap became a published number with a deadline

**Date:** 2026-08-25 · **Confidence:** `[high]` on figures quoted to AR2026, `[derived]` where marked · **Source:** BHP FY2026 reporting suite published 18 August 2026, refreshed against the 12 August dossier `/Verterra Collaboration/Bank and institution exploration/RESEARCH-bhp-use-case-dossier.md`. Full handover in `/Commercial Module Console/research/REFRESH-implications.md`.

## The headline

BHP restated its Healthy Environment goal from "at least 30 per cent" to **at least 2 million hectares** under conservation, restoration or regenerative practices by FY2030, and published progress for the first time: **246,000 ha, 3.8 per cent** (AR2026 pp.44, 50). FY2025 was ~98,000 ha `[derived]`. Almost the entire FY2026 increase of ~148,000 ha came from **one project, Jackboot in South Australia**.

They need roughly **1.75 million more hectares in four years.** FY2027 milestone is 42 per cent of goal hectares in plan or under practices, from 12 per cent today. **Natural capital accounts: one today (BMA, CSIRO peer-reviewed), three required by FY2027.**

BHP also published its land total for the first time: **7.3 million hectares owned, leased or managed**, of which ~154,500 ha disturbed and ~22,600 ha under rehabilitation. Much of the estate is pastoral grazing and AgriProve's method basis is grazing land.

**Why this matters.** The 12 August dossier predicted BHP's own estate was the larger contract but rested on a soft "statement of unmet demand". Both sides of that equation are now published figures with a deadline attached.

**The caveat that keeps us honest.** The goal is scoped to land BHP *stewards*. Module hectares on third-party grazing land do not count toward it. This is an argument for work on their estate, not a way to inflate what a module delivers.

## The compliance case shrank

BHP now discloses ~848,000 credits surrendered for FY2025 compliance, a **US$23m BHP-share / US$33m 100%-basis** obligation, US$71m of carbon credit assets, and a forward ceiling **under US$75m per annum at BHP share to FY2030**. Cost is audited, published and modest. **Any urgency framing built on cost is dead.** Drop the ~A$17m Carbon Pulse figure. BHP carries roughly 70 per cent of the obligation, so the 100 per cent basis overstates exposure by about a third.

## What replaced it, and it is better

Three FY2026 disclosures (AR2026 §5.2.7 p.85, Tables 6a/6b):
- "BHP may also sell carbon credits ... or **originate carbon credits through project development or direct investment**"
- Voluntary credits for the long-term goal marked **"(expected)"**
- CY2050 residual needing offset quantified for the first time at **3 to 5 MtCO2-e a year**

And **US$49m of prepayments for future delivery of carbon credits** on the balance sheet, up from US$32m. **BHP already forward-contracts credit delivery**, which is structurally what a commercial module is.

Still binding: BHP will **not** use regulatory credits, including Safeguard ACCUs and SMCs, when determining whether it has achieved its FY2030 target. Never pitch against that target.

## Framework position, corrected

**TNFD is not adopted** (four mentions in 248 pages, all definitional). **GRI 101 Biodiversity is not disclosed.** Do not build for this buyer on either. Their frame is natural capital accounting on the CSIRO Natural Capital Handbook, where they are behind their own milestone.

**Assurance.** EY gave **reasonable assurance over Scope 1 and 2** for FY2026, beyond the ASSA 5010 year-one floor of limited, plus limited over the rest including Scope 3. BHP has chosen to be audited harder than required. FY2029 reasonable assurance over everything with the liability shield expired stands as a fact of ASSA 5010. No BHP statement about FY2027 plans was established; do not invent one.

**AASB S2 para 36(e):** the requirement is verified in the standard; whether BHP's disclosure maps to it is **not established**. Cite the requirement, never their compliance with it.

## Figures to stop using

FY2025 Scope 1+2 of 8.7 Mt / minus 36% is restated to **9.3 Mt / minus 34%**, FY2020 baseline now 14.1 Mt. FY2030 headroom is **0.47 Mt** and FY2026 emissions rose to 9.4 Mt. "BHP publishes no consolidated land total" is dead. Beenup and Olympic Dam as natural capital pilots are superseded by BMA; Beenup is not mentioned once in AR2026.

## Traps

1. **BHP's climate governance page still names Dr Fiona Wild** as VP Sustainability and Climate Change. She moved to Westpac, reported March 2025. The page carries a 2026 footer and was not corrected. Do not brief from it, and reconfirm any VP name before use.
2. AR2026 states 13 per cent of disturbed land is under rehabilitation; 22,600 ÷ 154,500 is **14.6 per cent**. Do not repeat 13 per cent unqualified.
3. Do not quote 6.47m ha as BHP's nature-goal denominator; it is derived from 246,000 ÷ 3.8%.
