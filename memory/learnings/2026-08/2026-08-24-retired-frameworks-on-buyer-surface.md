# Four retired or superseded frameworks reached a buyer-facing surface

**Date:** 2026-08-24 · **Confidence:** `[high]` · **Source:** Commercial module console Credits tab review, Cowork thread. Detail in `/Commercial Module Console/briefs/BRIEF-credits-tab.md`.

## What happened

The Credits tab carried framework tags on each credit line as the argument for why the credits mattered. Seven of those claims did not stand up:

| On the tab | Why it failed |
|---|---|
| TCFD | Disbanded 2023, monitoring transferred to the ISSB from 2024. Also a climate framework placed on a nature instrument |
| Climate Active | Australian Government decided to close the programme; consultation paper July 2026, transition of 12 or 18 months after a decision expected before end of 2026 |
| GRI 304 | Superseded by GRI 101: Biodiversity 2024, effective 1 January 2026 |
| Reef 2050 | A Commonwealth and Queensland water quality policy plan, not a corporate reporting standard |
| Scope 1 on plantings ACCUs, Scope 3 on Reef Credits | An offset is not an emissions reduction. Category errors |
| "the single largest gap in your TNFD submission, at 8 of 14 unanswered" | Asserts a fact about the buyer's own filing that we cannot see |

Four of seven named a standard that was retired, superseded or being wound up.

## The mechanism, which is the useful part

The tab was asked to lead with the obligation each credit closes rather than the volume available. It could not, because the obligations it pointed at did not hold. **You cannot lead with an argument that is not true**, so it decorated the cards instead and introduced a colour palette that did not exist elsewhere in the product. The visual symptom was downstream of a factual defect.

## Two rules that came out of it

1. Verify every named external standard at build time, not from recall. See `../../state/rules.md` 2026-08-24.
2. When a brief supplies illustrative placeholder figures alongside an instruction to keep claims provisional, the placeholders get built as real. A placeholder looks unfinished and a specific number looks finished, so the specific number wins. Either withhold the illustrative figures or make the provisional state a visible design element.

## Second-order cost

The console's main asset is that a compliance professional trusts the Delivery tab. The marketplace sits one click away. A single checkable falsehood on the marketplace spends credibility on the tab that matters most.
