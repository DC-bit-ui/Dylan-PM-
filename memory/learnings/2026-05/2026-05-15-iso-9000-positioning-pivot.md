# ISO 9000 / regulatory-grade data positioning — value-prop pivot

**Source.** Hobbs interview, 2026-05-15. Highest-signal capture of the conversation, worth propagating beyond his profile.

## The problem this solves
Conventional farmers — those following agronomist/chemical-company recipes, treating soil as a growing medium — genuinely doubt they can lift soil carbon materially. The *"we do this anyway"* objection is real for this segment. Trying to fudge it on a credits-only pitch destroys credibility on the visit.

## The pivot
For this segment, stop leading with carbon revenue. Lead with the **data asset**:
- AgriProve's measurement is **ISO 9001:2015 certified**.
- The data track record is what banks, insurers, and investment houses are increasingly **requiring** to underwrite agricultural lending and risk products.
- The carbon credit becomes the by-product. The compliance-grade data is the structural value.
- A farmer who would have walked away from a credits pitch can engage with this framing because it answers a question they actually have ("what's my farm worth to a bank in 5 years?").

## Why: How to apply
**Use when:** The prospect raises the "we do this anyway" objection, OR is conventional-farming with low practice-change appetite, OR mentions financing/refinancing/succession in discovery.

**Don't use when:** Practice-change-curious prospects who are already engaged on the credit side — leading with data asset there mutes the more immediate hook (revenue).
