## The validated pattern (capture this as a Ben signature move)

Ben has converged on a specific cold-open framing that's producing "very short" calls ending in farm-visit bookings. Verbatim from him:

- **Curiosity frame:** *"He's keen to see how we do it over here"* (Hobbs-from-America positioning, but reframed so the prospect feels honoured rather than pitched at).
- **NOT this:** *"We want to show him how it's done"* — Ben explicitly avoided this as "a bit arrogant."
- **Sourcing question handler:** when prospects ask "how did you find me?" — *"It's part of the regional outreach program... your property has come up as one that's, you know, getting good results and doing good things... contacting Riverina Enterprises that are doing good things."*
- **Credibility marker:** *"We're Aubrey based... very much helps in the beginning."* Geography substitutes for company.
- **Tone commitment:** *"Padded conversation,"* *"fence line chat,"* *"very lean back, not sort of too intense."*
- **The "take it to the team" confidence shield** (Ben got this from Harry early on): when a prospect raises something technical Ben can't field, *"I prefer to give you the right information and take it back to the team."* Lets him stay in the conversation without overclaiming expertise.

## Why it works (Ben's read of his own pattern)

> "I think playing on that a little bit is why I'm getting some easy — not easy, but the conversation is very short, and it's a yep, and it's a booking."

Plus the implicit compliment ("your property has come up as one that's getting good results") puts the power on the prospect. They feel selected, not solicited.
