## Why this learning exists

The original Cowork handoff (filed 2026-05-11) summarised these clauses and the summaries contained ≥5 substantive interpretation errors (definition of "Competitive Business", scope of cl 21.1.6, scope of cl 21.1.2, geographic fallback, scope of cl 21.1.1). The verbatim re-read corrected these. **Future references must cite verbatim text, not summaries.**

Generalisable lesson: when a complex legal / contractual document is summarised by an automated system, the summary will frequently strip limiter language. Always demand verbatim for anything that will govern downstream automated decisions.

## Key clauses governing career-portfolio activity

### cl 13.1 — Exclusive service

> Devote the whole of your working time, attention and skill to the faithful performance of your duties... must not be engaged in any other activity unless you have the written approval of the Company.

Implication: career-portfolio activity is defensible as personal record-keeping (analogous to maintaining a CV / LinkedIn profile), not "engagement in another activity" — provided it runs on personal infrastructure outside work hours and contains no AgriProve IP / Confidential Information. Full defence at [`../../../inbox/cowork/2026-05-12-cl-13-1-defensibility.md`](../../../inbox/cowork/2026-05-12-cl-13-1-defensibility.md).

### cl 19 — Confidentiality (perpetual)

Comprehensive. Covers "all trade secrets, industrial processes, intellectual property, or any information concerning the business, affairs, or finances of the Company" plus 8 specific sub-categories. cl 19.1.4 explicitly covers "all data, know how, algorithms and technology." cl 19.1.8 covers LinkedIn-stored client lists. Survives termination (cl 19.8). Breach = serious misconduct, summary dismissal (cl 19.9).

### cl 20 — Intellectual Property (total assignment with limiters)

> Company owns absolutely... all Intellectual Property made, discovered or created by you (whether alone or in conjunction with others, and whether created outside your normal place of work or during or outside standard working hours).

Limiters (20.1.1–20.1.4):
- 20.1.1 "in the course of, or as a consequence of, performing your duties"
- 20.1.2 "using the Group's or any client's premises, resources or facilities"
- 20.1.3 "directly or indirectly as a result of your access to the Confidential Information"
- 20.1.4 "in respect of, or associated with, any of the Company's services"

cl 20.5 prohibits publishing IP under assignment.

**Implication for portfolio:** limiters exist but 20.1.3 and 20.1.4 are broad. Portfolio content must be at a high abstraction (role-level professional description) so it doesn't itself constitute IP. Specific work product, methodology descriptions, or anything "in respect of, or associated with" company services is in scope of assignment and cannot be published. **Apex sanitiser implements a "Cl 20 IP reframe pass" — any "I built / designed / invented / created" framing for work product is reframed to "I led / managed / contributed to" before promotion.**

### cl 21 — Post-employment restraint (3 months, Australia)

Five sub-clauses (21.1.1–21.1.6):
- 21.1.1 Non-compete — **with exception**: permitted if role does not involve similar work
- 21.1.2 Non-solicit (Key Clients with whom Dylan had dealings in prior 12 months)
- 21.1.3 Non-solicit (employees with whom Dylan had dealings in prior 12 months)
- 21.1.4 Non-solicit (contractors with whom Dylan had dealings in prior 12 months)
- 21.1.5 Procuring breach
- 21.1.6 Cannot accept employment that **requires** revealing / basing judgement upon / using Confidential Information — this is NOT "inevitable disclosure" (narrower legal standard)

Restraint Period: 3 months from termination (cl 21.3). Geographic scope: "anywhere within Australia" (cl 21.1) — no cascading fallback.

"Competitive Business" is **used but not defined** in the document — legally weaker, generally favorable to Dylan.
