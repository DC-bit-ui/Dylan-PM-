## cl 13.1 — Exclusive Service

> "devote the whole of your working time and attention to the business of the Company… and you must not be engaged in any other activity unless you have the written approval of the Company"

**Implication:** All portfolio work must be conducted outside working hours on personal devices. No written approval has been sought or granted for portfolio activity.

## cl 19 — Confidential Information (perpetual)

**Definition (cl 19.1):** "all trade secrets, industrial processes, intellectual property, or any information concerning the business, affairs, or finances of the Company" — plus 8 specific categories including:
- cl 19.1.4: "All data, know how, algorithms and technology associated with the Company's operations"
- cl 19.1.5: "Client's records"
- cl 19.1.6: "Client lists and names of client contacts"
- cl 19.1.8: Client lists and contacts developed during employment but stored on external platforms including LinkedIn, Twitter, Facebook

**Obligation (cl 19.2):** Perpetual — during and after employment.

**Prohibited acts (cl 19.3):** Must not publish or divulge to any person; must not use for any purpose other than the Company's.

**Breach = serious misconduct (cl 19.9):** May be dismissed without notice.

**Return on termination (cl 19.10):** All papers, data, records, documents relating to Confidential Information.

## cl 20 — Intellectual Property (total assignment)

**Scope (cl 20.1):** Company owns all IP created during employment — including outside normal hours and outside the workplace — if created:
- in the course of or as a consequence of performing duties (cl 20.1.1)
- using company or client resources (cl 20.1.2)
- directly or indirectly as a result of access to Confidential Information (cl 20.1.3)
- in respect of or associated with any of the Company's services (cl 20.1.4)

**Publishing prohibition (cl 20.5):** "You shall not do or omit to do anything which will or may result in imperilling the Intellectual Property… including without limitation, publishing any of it."

**Perpetual disclosure duty (cl 20.2):** "Irrespective of the termination of the employment" — must disclose IP that may benefit the Company.

## cl 21 — Post-Employment Restraint

**Restraint Period (cl 21.3):** 3 months from termination.

**Geographic scope (cl 21.1):** "anywhere within Australia for the Restraint Period." No cascading fallback to state/territory or 50km radius exists in the document.

**Non-compete with exception (cl 21.1.1):** Employment at a competitor is permitted if "the capacity in which you are so employed or provide services does not involve you performing work of a similar nature to that performed by you in the course of your employment with the Company."

**Non-solicitation (cl 21.1.2):** Scoped to "Key Clients with whom you had dealings in the 12-month period prior to termination" — NOT all clients/customers/suppliers.

**Employment requiring CI use (cl 21.1.6):** "accept any employment or render any services that would **require** you to reveal, base judgement upon, or otherwise use any Confidential Information referred to in clause 19." This is a "require" threshold — NOT "inevitable disclosure." The term "inevitable disclosure" does not appear in the document.

**"Competitive Business" — UNDEFINED:** The term is used in cl 21.1 but never defined anywhere in the Letter of Offer. Its scope is subject to contextual interpretation. Do not assume it is narrow (soil carbon measurement only) or impossibly broad.

## ⚠️ 5 Interpretation Shifts (corrections to handoff brief)

These correct inaccuracies in `inbox/cowork/2026-05-11-claude-code-handoff.md` and `inbox/cowork/2026-05-11-policy-review-draft.md`. The verbatim clauses file is authoritative.

1. **"Competitive Business" is NOT defined.** The handoff stated it = soil carbon measurement/quantification/ACCU issuance. That text does not appear in the document. The term is used but never defined.

2. **cl 21.1.1 has a critical exception.** The handoff omitted the "except where" clause. Employment at a competitor is permitted if the role does not involve similar work.

3. **cl 21.1.6 is NOT "inevitable disclosure."** The handoff described it as such. The actual text says "require you to reveal, base judgement upon, or otherwise use" — a higher threshold than inevitable disclosure. The term "inevitable disclosure" does not appear in the document.

4. **cl 21.1.2 is scoped to Key Clients with prior dealings.** The handoff paraphrased this as covering "all clients/customers/suppliers." It does not.

5. **No geographic cascading fallback.** The handoff referenced "cascading fallback: state, 50km." No such text exists. Geographic scope is "anywhere within Australia" — full stop.
