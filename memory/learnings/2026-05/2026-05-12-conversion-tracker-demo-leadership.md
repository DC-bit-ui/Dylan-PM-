# 2026-05-12 — Conversion Tracker demo to Will + Kieren

## What happened
First leadership demo of the Stormboy Conversion Tracker dashboard to Will and Kieren. Walked them through Plays, Patterns, Recent wins, Loss analysis, LawrieCo learnings. Will engaged actively, asked methodological questions. Kieren listened. The session ended with Will and Kieren both saying yes to continued investment, conditional on the work tying back to the 30,000 hectare conversion priority.

## What landed (use this framing again)

- **The conversion delta as headline:** pre-Stormboy median 140d → current 51d. They engaged with this number immediately. Stat doesn't need any setup.
- **LawrieCo as proof of concept for cross-source learning:** 5.1× win rate, 40% faster close. Will said variations of "we know this, but as this evolves over time I think it becomes quite a valuable aspect." That's not a "wow" — it's a "you're not wasting my time, keep going."
- **Calling out the in-progress nature** ("this is its very early demo stage, but as it gets more information it will become smarter") let them grade the work on trajectory not finished state.

## What didn't land

- **Breadth without focus is the failure mode.** I drove through too many surfaces in 25 minutes. Self-correction mid-meeting: *"the interface is a little bit clunky and hard to use; I want to simplify it for broader rollout — v2 will tighten scope."* Pre-empted the criticism but it was already visible.
- **Will's classification-accuracy question is a real concern.** He pushed on "how do you know it's classifying this correctly?" The honest answer that the signal-to-noise filter is part of the design didn't fully convince — and shouldn't, because the *artifact-vs-heuristic gap* (discovered hours later when wiring engagement timelines into exemplar cards) proved Will right. Heuristics were quietly miscategorising leads.

## Leadership constraint to keep top-of-mind

> The absolute priority is 30,000 hectare conversion. Everything tailored toward Storm Boy and conversion. Snapshot automation + KCT have become the only priorities.

EIH work is **backlogged** even though invested in. Not "deferred to next quarter" — backlogged. Treat any future product work the same way: if it's not directly aiding the 30K, it doesn't ship now.

## The framing that earned the "yes"

Dylan: *"For me, I see this as conversion-related — it's going to feed into the Claude code processes that Hobbs and Ben are doing. Starting to create some intelligence systems that aid the Storm Boy piece."*

This is the language that bought continued investment. Keep using it: **collective intelligence system that learns from the team and digitises Hobbs's approach to feed Storm Boy conversion.** Not "dashboard." Not "analytics." Not "PM tool." It's a sales enablement system.
