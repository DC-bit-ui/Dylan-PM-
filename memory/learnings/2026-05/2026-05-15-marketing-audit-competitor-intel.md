# Marketing audit findings + Carbon Link in-region intel

**Source:** Stormboy standup 2026-05-15. Audit by Daniel (Marketing). Competitor intel from team observation.

## The competitive picture

- **Carbon Link is actively door-knocking in the Storm Boy region.** Not just digital marketing — physical outbound presence in AgriProve's strongest territory.
- **Carbon Link is hiring a BDM / Carbon Projects role explicitly targeting Queensland.** Direct geographic pressure where AgriProve has the largest concentration of converted hectares.
- Bill Hyem's old territory (the QLD heartland) is exactly where this lands.

## Daniel's audit — what competitors lean on (and where AP has drifted)

| Communication pattern | Competitor approach (Carbon Link et al.) | AgriProve current state |
|---|---|---|
| In-person touchpoints + referrals | **Primary motion** | Drifted away from — has become digital-funnel-heavy |
| Financial / cost-benefit messaging ("no upfront costs", direct $ on hectares) | **Lead with this** | Has drifted to platform / methodology emphasis instead |
| "Does your offering meet *my* needs" framing | Strong | Heavy on volume/credentials (e.g. "57 projects, 22,689 ACCUs, $800K") |

The customer-side feedback signal: **farmers respond more to fit-framing than credential-framing.** AgriProve's strongest credential statements aren't moving the needle as much as the team thought.

## Tech/platform validation (from same audit)

The good news Daniel surfaced: AgriProve's platform IS genuinely superior to competitors on tech depth. The story is real; the storytelling has drifted.

## Why: How to apply

**Why this matters:** the Messaging tab's "current campaign" sub-tab is now the highest-leverage open thread on the dashboard. The audit gives the team a clear thesis on where copy needs to shift:
1. **Re-anchor on in-person + referral motion** — borrow language from Hobbs's farm-visit transcripts (the verbatim language bank in his profile)
2. **Lead with financial framing** — Bill Hyem's 3-touch outbound used this exactly (peer case study with dollar outcome upfront — *"Karen and Rob Scanlan earned 3,559 credits valued at over $124,000"*). Bill's playbook is already the right playbook
3. **Stop leading with credentials** — push 57 projects / 22K ACCUs to the proof layer, not the hook
