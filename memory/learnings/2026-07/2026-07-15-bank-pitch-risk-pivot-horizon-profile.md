# Bank pitch pivots to risk-led: regulatory disclosure wedge + "Horizon Profile" artifact

**Date:** 2026-07-15 · **Source:** Workshop (Matthew, Dylan, Hobbs) `notes.granola.ai/t/2331a99e-...` + 3 deliverables (Rabobank deck, Commercial Structures, rundown) · **Confidence:** [high]

## The pivot
The bank pitch moved from "sell credits/offtake" to **solving the bank's regulatory risk-assessment problem** with AgriProve's data/tech. Wedge: mandatory Climate-Related Financial Disclosure (live 1 Jan 2025) + AASB S2 physical-climate-risk + APRA capital adequacy + TNFD require banks to quantify physical climate risk across ag books — **but the measurement infrastructure doesn't exist.** AgriProve = that infrastructure. Aspirin, not vitamin: the CRO buys capital efficiency + defensible disclosure, not ecology.

## The artifact (Dylan's next build)
**"Horizon Profile"** (working title, Matthew's pref) — an interactive **single-property ecological risk profile** concept: headline risk score + direction-of-travel (R/A/G, 5-yr), **ecological default probability** (Monte Carlo) as a lead indicator of *financial* default 3–5 yrs early, SON velocity, photosynthetic efficiency, effective rainfall capture, **counterfactual** (property vs do-nothing vs neighbour vs region), rainfall-normalised benchmarking + 9-yr stress history, forward projection, one-click **AASB S2 / TNFD disclosure export**. Portfolio→property triage view is the CRO's jaw-drop (sketch first, build later per Dylan). Positioning: *"a credit-rating terminal for farmland ecological health."*

## Concept focus (refined 2026-07-15, Dylan)
The concept = **risk profiling of the agricultural businesses** (Hobbs' angle): show the bank which businesses in its book / pipeline are on a declining trajectory before impairments, and answer its physical-climate disclosure. A property can be **profiled remotely (no farmer sign-up required)** — that's the pure analytics-infrastructure play. **NOT "us bringing them projects/farmers"** — that customer-growth angle is a secondary, later upside, not the concept. Commercialise via risk-profiling-as-a-service (MaaS ~$10k/property), capital/ICAAP positioning, and risk-priced lending; credit offtake/establishment/CEFC are adjacent/later.
