# Framing: AgriProve = "natural capital analytics service provider," not "soil carbon project developer"

**Date:** 2026-07-15 · **Source:** Dylan direct · **Type:** framing preference (apply going forward) · **Confidence:** [high]

## The rule
When describing AgriProve — especially in bank/institutional/external pitch contexts — refer to it as a **natural capital analytics service provider** (the measurement + risk-intelligence infrastructure layer), NOT a "soil carbon project developer" / "soil carbon company."

**Why:** the "soil carbon" descriptor pulls the reader (and AI drafts) toward the carbon-credit-origination frame, which undercuts the risk-led pitch where AgriProve's value is being the *analytics/measurement infrastructure* a bank needs for physical-climate-risk disclosure. Carbon and reef credits are **one output and revenue line** within the deal, not the company's identity.

## How to apply
- Identity/positioning line: "natural capital analytics service provider" (or "natural capital analytics + risk intelligence").
- Credits/projects: keep as mechanism/output where relevant, but never as the lead identity.
- The product the bank buys = analytics + risk intelligence + disclosure-ready outputs; the ERF carbon/reef projects are the delivery vehicle beneath.
