# Bank engagement strategy — reef hook, MDB business prize, ACWIS compounding

**Date:** 2026-07-14 · **Source:** Dylan direct (session on the bank pitch deck) · **Confidence:** [high — Dylan articulated]

## The strategy (locked for the bank pitch)
Three layers, in order:
1. **Hook (hearts):** the Great Barrier Reef — our generation's most visible climate wound. Wins the bank's *new retail and youth* customers ("conscious banking").
2. **Business prize (book growth):** new agri lending + new customers across **both the reef catchments and the Murray-Darling Basin** — Australia's two great agricultural water systems. National, not single-catchment.
3. **Compounding engine:** as Verterra's **ACWIS** water-quality-credit capability expands, the bank's book grows with it → the bank becomes **"the bank for water-quality credits in Australia."**

The green **establishment loan** is the hook: recruits *new* farmers into projects and wins them as **lifelong customers across all lending** (home, machinery, operating, succession), nationally.

## Key corrections to prior framing
- **De-weight the existing book.** AgriProve's ~47-enterprise / ~11,750 ha in-region portfolio is **real** (current book) but is a **warm start / credibility proof, NOT the centrepiece**. The pitch is forward-looking: new business, new customers, new projects recruited.
- **Drop the "tender / one winner per catchment / auction" framing** the v2 deck invented. Keep competitive urgency only as *first-mover category leadership*.
- Name: **kill "Compact."** Program name should span clean water farm-to-reef-and-river (reef-only undersells the MDB prize); reef stays the emotional consumer line. Options explored: Source to Sea, The Water Standard, Clean Country, Watershed.

## Deck deep-dive verdict (v2, "Reef Catchment Compact")
Aesthetically strong (deep teal / ivory serif / coral; 5-act narrative; good counterfactual + bleaching visuals; banker-credible LVR/valuer language; "four budget owners = can't say no"; greenwashing-proof moat). Weaknesses: opaque name, over-weighted book + tender framing (now corrected above), reef emotion arrives too late, slides 2/3/4/7 too dense (one-idea-per-slide fix needed).
