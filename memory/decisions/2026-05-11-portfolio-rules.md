## The 9 Portfolio Rules

### Rule 1: Do not disclose proprietary information
Do not disclose proprietary methodologies, SOC models, geotagged data, algorithms, know-how, technology specifics, customer contract terms, financials, ACCU volumes, or project yields.
— *cl 19.1, 19.1.4, 19.3.1, 19.8, 20.1; DRAFT Protected Information Policy s3.1; DRAFT Access Policy PROPRIETARY class*

### Rule 2: Do not include personal information
Do not include personal information of customers, employees, or stakeholders.
— *Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) APPs; Privacy Policy POLPRV; DRAFT Access Policy s3.1; cl 19.1.5, 19.1.6*

### Rule 3: Do not claim to speak for AgriProve
Do not claim to speak for AgriProve or represent company positions.
— *HSE003 s5.3 (signed, binding); Delegation of Authority POLDoA s16; cl 13.2*

### Rule 4: Describe role and outcomes using public information only
Do describe your role, responsibilities, and outcomes using only publicly available information. General industry knowledge, public company information, and personal professional skills are safe. "I managed a product roadmap for a soil carbon measurement platform" = safe. "The roadmap included [specific features/timelines]" = Confidential.
— *Safe harbour: cl 19.1 does not extend to general professional knowledge or publicly available information*

### Rule 5: Do not publish work product
Do not publish any work product created during employment — PRDs, code, design specs, data models, research outputs are assigned IP.
— *cl 20.1 (total assignment, including outside hours/workplace), cl 20.5 (publishing prohibition)*

### Rule 6: Anonymise all case studies
Anonymise all case studies — remove identifiable customer, property, or project details. Do not reference specific client relationships, including LinkedIn connections.
— *cl 19.1.5, 19.1.6, 19.1.8 (explicitly covers LinkedIn), 19.6; Privacy Policy POLPRV; DRAFT Protected Information Policy s3.1*

### Rule 7: Reference regulatory framework as professional credentials
Reference the regulatory framework (CFI Act, ERF, Carbon Industry Code, AFSL) as professional credentials without disclosing internal compliance details.
— *Public legislation and codes are safe; AFSL internal compliance = Confidential per cl 19.1*

### Rule 8: Conduct portfolio work outside working hours on personal devices
Conduct all portfolio work outside working hours on personal devices and infrastructure.
— *cl 13.1 (exclusive service); DRAFT AUP s1.1–1.4, s4.2.8–4.2.10*

### Rule 9: Post-employment restraint awareness
Be aware of the 3-month post-employment restraint (cl 21). Avoid creating portfolio content that could evidence that future employment would *require* use of Confidential Information (cl 21.1.6). Frame expertise as "product management in regulated climate-tech" not "soil carbon measurement and quantification." Demonstrate breadth (PM, strategy, stakeholder management) not depth in competitive domain. Note: "Competitive Business" is used but NOT defined in the Letter of Offer.
— *cl 21.1, 21.1.1 (non-compete with exception), 21.1.6 ("require" threshold, not "inevitable disclosure"), 21.3 (3-month Restraint Period)*
