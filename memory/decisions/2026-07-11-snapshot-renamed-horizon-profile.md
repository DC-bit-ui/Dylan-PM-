**Date:** 2026-07-11
**Confidence:** [high] — from Granola meeting transcripts this week

## Decision

The product previously called "Snapshot" is now officially named **"HORIZON Profile"**.

Kieren and Matthew are aligned on this rename. All internal references, product copy, and communications should use "HORIZON Profile" going forward.
