## Captures (organised by interview question)

### 🎯 Transition Cues — when to introduce carbon
- **30–60 minutes of rapport building** before carbon is mentioned. Hobbs reads the room and waits until *"the social climate has been properly lubricated"*.
- ~**50% of the time, customers bring up carbon themselves** — Hobbs lets this happen naturally rather than forcing the transition.
- **NEW pattern being tested:** a direct opener — *"Have you guys looked into the carbon program at all?"* — is generating positive responses. Suggests the strict 30-60min rule is loosening when other rapport signals are strong.

### 🔍 Close Detection — what tells him a deal is going to convert
- **The "Ben books → Hobbs visits" funnel is the clearest close signal.** All signed KCTs have followed this exact flow.
- Customers **introduced to AgriProve by Ben first** arrive more carbon-oriented — they're pre-warmed by the phone conversation. This validates the two-motion separation in the dashboard architecture.
- **Field day social proof is a strong close accelerant.** Concrete framing he uses: 7 farmers at a typical field day, 3 of them have carbon projects, 2 of those 3 are with AgriProve. Seeing credited/in-process projects in person removes doubt.
- **Post-visit speed matters.** Delivering Horizon Snapshot + maps **within 1 day** keeps momentum — customers *"want to see the money"*. Slow delivery loses the moment.

### ⚠️ Additionality Weakness — the hardest objection
- *"We do this anyway"* is the killer. Conventional farmers follow agronomist/chemical-company recipes, treat soil as a growing medium, and genuinely doubt they can increase soil carbon materially.
- Hobbs's honest assessment: **these farmers need real practice changes to generate carbon beyond 1–2 issuances.** Trying to fudge this loses credibility.
- **NEW positioning pivot** (highest-signal capture of the interview): shift the value prop for these prospects to **regulatory-grade ISO 9000 validated data**. Banks, insurers, and investment houses require this kind of soil-data track record. The credit is a by-product; the **data asset** is the real value. This opens the door for conventional farmers who couldn't be convinced on credits alone.

### 🛑 What Hobbs Has Stopped Doing
- Stopped leading with carbon as the cold-open. The direct question (*"Have you guys looked into the carbon program at all?"*) is a recent evolution, not his default.
- Moved away from single-touchpoint follow-up. The multi-touch post-visit cadence (maps → economics → handoff to Ben) is now standard.

### ❌ What Ben Gets Wrong
- **Lacks agricultural background.** Hobbs is direct: this cannot be faked and destroys trust with farmers on visits. Ben's strengths are real but they're better suited to a different surface.
- Ben is **a traditional salesman, not an agrarian connector** — fundamentally different approach needed.
- **Ben's right surface is phone appointments**, not on-farm visits. The dashboard's two-motion separation actually maps to this: Motion 1 (Storm Boy outreach via phone) is Ben's lane; Motion 2 (existing pipeline farm visits) is Hobbs's.
- Current numbers: Ben missing weekly call target (target 65, achieved 46). Hobbs hit 23 of his 20 visit target.

### 📚 What's Teachable to the Next Person

Three non-negotiable traits for farm-visit success:
1. **Legitimate agricultural background** — cannot be simulated.
2. **Outgoing personality with charisma / X-factor** — there's no script that compensates.
3. **Performance under pressure** — there are no do-overs on farm visits. A flubbed first visit doesn't get rerun.

**Recommended reading for the next-Hobbs:** *How to Win Friends and Influence People* — Hobbs sees this as the foundational text.

**Study material from the canon:** transcripts of his successful KCT flows — specifically **John Harpley** and possibly **Georgie Millard**.
