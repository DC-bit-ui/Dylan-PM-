# Cl 13.1 — Defensibility Analysis (Career-Portfolio Activity)

**Date:** 2026-05-12. **Status:** part of compliance gate evidence. Reference document, not a separate sign-off.

## The clause

Letter of Offer (220615) cl 13.1:

> Devote the whole of your working time, attention and skill to the faithful performance of your duties... and must not be engaged in any other activity unless you have the written approval of the Company.

## The question

Does the Apex career-capture system constitute "engagement in any other activity" requiring written approval from AgriProve?

## The analysis: defensibly NO, this is personal record-keeping

| Factor | Why it supports record-keeping characterisation |
|---|---|
| **No counter-party** | No client of Dylan exists; no external party is served by this activity |
| **No income / commercial benefit** | Not a business or consultancy; no second income generated |
| **Personal infrastructure** | Cowork runs on Dylan's personal Windows machine; personal Notion workspace; personal Claude account; personal integration tokens |
| **Personal time** | Apex captures fire at 18:00 SAST weekdays — after working hours. Processing of work-day signal is administrative, analogous to writing a journal of one's professional development |
| **Analogous to universally accepted practices** | Maintaining a CV, LinkedIn profile, training records, professional certifications — all universally accepted as non-violating "other activity". The career portfolio is more systematic but conceptually identical |
| **No detraction from work** | Fully automated capture (no Dylan time during work hours); ~5 min/week audit-digest glance is admin time |
| **No additional company resource use beyond existing approved flows** | Reads via Cowork's existing MCP connectors (Granola / Outlook / Teams / Jira / Confluence / Notion) — same access pattern already in operation for Apex Morning Briefing and EOD Reconciliation |
| **Conservative content scope** | 9 Portfolio Rules + sanitiser strip anything that could constitute work product, IP under cl 20, or Confidential Information under cl 19 |
| **No external publication of company information** | Personal Notion is private. Personal Claude reads sanitised Portfolio only. External use (CV / LinkedIn / interview) goes through Dylan's own composition step, not auto-published |

## If challenged

> "I maintain personal career records, analogous to a CV or LinkedIn profile. The process runs on my personal equipment outside work hours and contains no AgriProve IP or Confidential Information. It is the same activity any employee performs when updating their CV after a work milestone — mine happens to be more systematic."

## Cross-coverage

This analysis also addresses:
- **Category 7** (External Communications) — portfolio outputs are personal, not company communications
- **Category 13** (Code of Conduct, HSE003 s5.3) — portfolio content does not represent AgriProve positions

## Optional escalation

Dylan may seek written approval from AgriProve People (Steve / equivalent) for additional comfort. Not required for activation. If escalation is preferred:

- Frame as transparency about an existing personal practice, not as a request for new authorisation
- Lead with: "I maintain a structured personal career record drawn from my work signal, governed by 9 confidentiality rules verified against Letter of Offer cl 19, 20, 21. It runs on personal infrastructure outside work hours. I'd like to flag it for the People file even though it's analogous to maintaining a CV."
- The conversation itself produces a useful datapoint about AgriProve's culture around career-development transparency

## What this analysis does NOT cover

- **Future architectural changes** (e.g., if Dylan starts using the portfolio commercially — freelance consulting, monetised content) — those would be "engagement in another activity" and require approval
- **Pre-launch / unannounced work** — Rule 6 strips this, but if a future use case requires sharing pre-launch info, re-review under cl 19.1
- **Post-employment use** — cl 21 restraints apply for 3 months post-termination; the Portfolio's existence is fine, but content choice should observe cl 21.1.6 (don't accept employment that requires using Confidential Information)
