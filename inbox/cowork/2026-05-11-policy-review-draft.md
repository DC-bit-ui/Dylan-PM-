## Key Letter of Offer Clauses (220615, signed)

### cl 13.1 — Exclusive Service
"You agree that you will devote the whole of your working time, attention and skill to the faithful performance of your duties... and must not be engaged in any other activity unless you have the written approval of the Company."

### cl 19 — Confidentiality (perpetual)
- cl 19.1: Confidential Information = "all trade secrets, industrial processes, intellectual property, or any information concerning the business, affairs, or finances of the Company"
- cl 19.1.4: "All data, know how, algorithms and technology associated with the Company's operations"
- cl 19.1.8: "Client lists... stored outside of the Company's technology platform, such as in Social Media/Professional Networking platforms, such as, but not limited to, LinkedIn"
- cl 19.2: Obligation exists "During the course of your employment... or after the termination of your employment"
- cl 19.3: Must not publish or divulge to any person
- cl 19.8: "Your obligations under this clause exist both during your employment and after your employment ceases"

### cl 20 — IP Assignment (total)
- cl 20.1: "the Company owns absolutely free from encumbrances all Intellectual Property made, discovered or created by you (whether alone or in conjunction with others, and whether created outside your normal place of work or during or outside standard working hours)"
- cl 20.5: "You shall not do or omit to do anything which will or may result in imperilling the Intellectual Property vesting in or being assigned to the Company... including without limitation, publishing any of it"

### cl 21 — Post-Employment Restraint
- cl 21.1: Cannot carry on or be engaged in any Competitive Business in Australia for the Restraint Period
- cl 21.1.6: Cannot accept employment requiring revealing/using Confidential Information or resulting in "inevitable disclosure"
- cl 21.2: "Competitive Business" = soil carbon measurement, quantification, and issuance of soil carbon units
- cl 21.3: Restraint Period = 3 months from termination
- cl 21.4: Geographic scope = anywhere in Australia (cascading fallback: state, 50km)

1. **Do not disclose** proprietary methodologies, SOC models, geotagged data, algorithms, know-how, technology specifics, customer contract terms, financials, ACCU volumes, or project yields (Letter of Offer cl 19.1.4, cl 20)
2. **Do not include** personal information of customers, employees, or stakeholders (Privacy Act 1988 + Privacy Policy POLPRV)
3. **Do not claim** to speak for AgriProve or represent company positions (HSE003 s5.3, DoA s16)
4. **Do describe** your role, responsibilities, and outcomes using only publicly available information
5. **Do not publish** any work product created during employment — PRDs, code, design specs, data models are assigned IP (Letter of Offer cl 20.1, cl 20.5)
6. **Anonymize** all case studies — remove identifiable customer, property, or project details. Do not reference specific client relationships, including LinkedIn connections (Letter of Offer cl 19.1.8)
7. **Reference** the regulatory framework (CFI Act, ERF, Carbon Industry Code, AFSL) as professional credentials without disclosing internal compliance details
8. **Conduct** all portfolio work outside working hours on personal devices and infrastructure (Letter of Offer cl 13.1; DRAFT AUP s1.1-1.4)
9. **Be aware** of 3-month post-employment restraint (Letter of Offer cl 21) — avoid creating portfolio content that could evidence "inevitable disclosure" of Confidential Information to a future employer (cl 21.1.6)
