# Pass 0 (variant) — Hobbs Farm-Visit Distillation

**Cadence:** every 2 days, only on new transcripts
**Model:** Haiku (per-transcript fan-out)
**Output cache:** `coaching/cache/farm_visit_distillates.json` (keyed by transcript_id)
**Consumed by:** B2 (Twins — what worked on-farm), A2 (Counter-Objection — what won at conversion stage), B1 (Risk Coach — Hobbs precedent)

## Why this exists separately from email Pass 0

The email Pass 0 is tuned for short bursts of correspondence. Hobbs farm-visit transcripts are different in shape:

- **Length:** 30–90 minutes of conversation (5,000–15,000 tokens raw)
- **Multi-topic:** customer raises several objections / value props in one sitting
- **Soft-sell context:** Hobbs (grazier-in-residence) is converting on-farm, not closing deals — his work is to make the customer comfortable enough to move into the AgriProve sales pipeline
- **Live tone shifts:** sentiment moves multiple times in one visit; need to track the trajectory, not just one shift
- **PII density:** customer names, property names, family context, financial conversations — must be generalised more aggressively than email distillation

This prompt produces multiple distillate records per transcript (one per major topic/objection) rather than one summary, so downstream prompts can reason at the topic level.

## Prompt

```
You are distilling a single Hobbs farm-visit transcript into structured
signal for downstream coaching. Hobbs is AgriProve's grazier-in-residence;
his job on these visits is to convert farmers softly toward soil carbon
projects under the ERF. He's not closing deals — he's making the customer
comfortable enough to move into the AgriProve sales pipeline. Calls and
outreach all funnel TO these on-farm visits.

# Context

AgriProve sells participation in 25-year soil carbon projects under
Australia's ERF. Revenue: 25/75 split (AgriProve 25%, customer 75%).
Alternative: fee-for-service. Customer pays upfront baseline-sampling
costs.

Storm Boy is a lead-acquisition campaign targeting farmers near Albury
NSW (Riverina cluster). On-farm visits are the conversion event. Post-
visit deliverable: a HORIZON Snapshot — customer report linking property
to ACCU revenue projection on their NRM region.

Common objection territories observed in HubSpot's closed_lost_reason
enum:
- 25/75 revenue split too high ("25%, too high")
- Fee-for-service preference (keep 100% of ACCUs)
- 25-year timeframe too long
- Upfront baseline costs too high
- Implementation commitment gap (the dominant catch-all)
- DIY preference ("Do it ourselves")
- Agronomy capability gap
- Eligibility / methodology fit
- Timing — not now
- Property life-cycle change
- Competitor chosen

Hypothesis territories that may surface on-farm but aren't in the enum:
- Carbon neutrality / measurement credibility
- ACCU market price doubts
- Trust / track record (will AgriProve be there in year 25?)
- Neighbour effect / disinformation

# Task

Read the transcript. Identify each MAJOR topic — typically 3-7 per
visit. For each topic, produce ONE distillate record. A "topic" is a
distinct objection, value prop, or question that Hobbs and the customer
discussed for at least a couple of minutes.

For each distillate:

1. topic_label — short tag from the territory list above, or a new
   territory if novel (≤60 chars)
2. customer_position — what the customer expressed (≤200 chars,
   PII-generalised)
3. hobbs_response — how Hobbs handled it (≤300 chars; specific framing,
   not platitude)
4. landed_or_friction — did the response land ("landed"), did it not
   ("friction"), or unclear ("ambiguous")
5. quotable_phrasing — if Hobbs used a turn of phrase that worked
   particularly well, capture it (≤200 chars). Empty string if not
   distinctive.
6. confidence — calibrated by signal strength

Then, ONE overall record per visit:

7. overall_outcome — "warming" | "neutral" | "cooling" | "ambiguous"
   based on net sentiment trajectory across the visit
8. one_line_summary — what the visit accomplished (≤200 chars)

# PII rules (strict — stricter than email Pass 0)

- NEVER echo customer names, family names, property names, exact
  hectares, exact dollar figures, neighbour names, or business names
  into the output
- GENERALISE: "5,000ha grazing operation in Riverina" not "the Smith
  family's Wagga property"; "low-mid 6-figure ACCU revenue scenario"
  not "$220,000"; "an experienced cattle producer with succession
  concerns" not "John, 67, planning to hand to his son"
- If a value prop or objection is tied to a specific case study Hobbs
  named, generalise the case to "[NRM region] case study" not the
  specific deal name

# Output rules

- Concise, signal-only. No commentary, no preamble.
- Strict JSON.
- One distillate record per topic; multiple per visit. Plus one
  visit_summary record.
- Use AgriProve language: ACCU, KCT, ERF, soil carbon project, NRM
  region, 25-year, 25/75 split, baseline sampling, HORIZON Snapshot.

# Output schema (strict)

{
  "version": "p0fv.1",
  "transcript_id": "<echo>",
  "visit_date": "<echo>",
  "region_nrm": "<echo or 'unknown'>",
  "size_bucket": "<echo or 'unknown'>",
  "topic_distillates": [
    {
      "topic_label": "<≤60 chars; territory-aligned where possible>",
      "customer_position": "<≤200 chars; PII-generalised>",
      "hobbs_response": "<≤300 chars; specific>",
      "landed_or_friction": "landed|friction|ambiguous",
      "quotable_phrasing": "<≤200 chars; or empty>",
      "confidence": "high|moderate|low"
    }
  ],
  "visit_summary": {
    "overall_outcome": "warming|neutral|cooling|ambiguous",
    "one_line_summary": "<≤200 chars; PII-generalised>"
  }
}
```

## Design notes

- **Multiple distillates per visit, not one.** Farm visits are multi-topic; collapsing to a single summary loses the per-objection precision A2 needs.
- **`quotable_phrasing` is the highest-leverage field.** If Hobbs has a turn of phrase that landed, that's directly transferable to other reps' framings. A2's `tactical_framing` field can quote these.
- **`landed_or_friction`** drives weighting in A2 — landed responses become "what won here"; friction responses become "what didn't work, and what the customer was actually concerned about."
- **Stricter PII guard than email Pass 0:** farm visits naturally contain more personal context (family, succession, finances). The model is instructed to generalise more aggressively here.
- **Cost estimate:** ~$0.005 per visit. ~50 visits over the past year ≈ $0.25 backfill, then a few cents per week.
- **Read-only relationship to Claudia's tool:** transcripts are sourced from her SharePoint area; we never write to those files. New distillates land in our cache only.
