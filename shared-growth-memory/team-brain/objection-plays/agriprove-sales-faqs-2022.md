Question

Response points

If we wish to, or are forced to offset our methane emissions in the future can we use the soil credits for ourselves?

Yes you would be able to use your own credits to offset your emissions if you chose.

When I get ACCUs, what are my options for what to do with them?

3 options 1) Can sell them at a fixed price (currently $12.50), 2) We can broker a sale on the spot market (at no extra cost to you), 3) you can hold them for future sale.

I have heard you can get biodiversity credits on top of soil carbon credits in the one project. Can you do that?

There are some state based programs such as the Land Restoration Fund that we have developed projects for in the past.

At this stage there is no national program for biodiversity credits.

Can the credits be held for longer than the 25 year period of the project?



What are the key changes in the 2021 method

1. Allowance of de-stocking during drought periods.

2. Reduced baseline accounting period from 10 years to 5 years.

3. Option for deep layers (below 30 cm) to be excluded from abatement calculations in early reporting rounds.

4. Broader definition of non-synthetic fertilisers.

5. Change in soil carbon stocks now measured relative to the previous sampling round (rather than using regression approach).

6. Change in soil carbon calculated using fixed mass accounting, rather than fixed depth.

7. Allowance for abatement accounting to be carried from one reporting period to the next.

8. Inclusion of net abatement and nitrogen emissions associated with cover crops.

9. Allowance of immaterial use of soil amendments containing coal and non-synthetic fertilisers and biochar that have not been sourced from a designated waste stream or carbon estimation area (CEA).

10. Broader definition of designated waste stream.

11. Changes in the irrigated area threshold relative to the baseline period (increased to 20%).

12. Removed restriction on non-synthetic fertiliser application within 24 months of a sampling round.

If the increase in SOC supports an increase in stocking numbers, does the project take into account the increase in methane emissions?



If we are in a bad drought at the end of the project and I am negative in SOC do I need to pay it back? If not, where is that written down so I am certain about it?





3. Questions on: Risks to the landholder

| Question | Response points |

| Is there an impact on potential future sub-division of land? | The project can only continue if production continues as stated in the Land Management Strategy. If you wish to develop a sub-division in the future you would need to purchase back credits that have been issued at the market rate including the credits that have been issued to Agriprove. |

| If the use of the modelled carbon credits is shown to be an overestimate at the next physical sampling is repayment required? If so, how would that be paid back? | No, there is just a reduced issuance of credits at the physical sampling point. |

| What happens to the project in the event that Agriprove folds or is sold? | If Agriprove is sold, the contracts continue under the new owner. If it folds, there is two pathways – the project is either signed over to you to continue or to another developer to manage for you (chosen by you). |

Question

Response points

Is there an impact on potential future sub-division of land?

The project can only continue if production continues as stated in the Land Management Strategy.

If you wish to develop a sub-division in the future you would need to purchase back credits that have been issued at the market rate including the credits that have been issued to Agriprove.

If the use of the modelled carbon credits is shown to be an overestimate at the next physical sampling is repayment required? If so, how would that be paid back?

No, there is just a reduced issuance of credits at the physical sampling point.

What happens to the project in the event that Agriprove folds or is sold?

If Agriprove is sold, the contracts continue under the new owner.

If it folds, there is two pathways – the project is either signed over to you to continue or to another developer to manage for you (chosen by you).



4. Soil carbon sampling (physical and remote)

| Question | Response points |

| We have had a couple of very good seasons and so carbon levels will be high at the moment, why should we not wait to baseline in a drought when it will drop again? | Carbon levels will certainly fluctuate with rainfall as it drives the production of carbon in the system and microbial populations. It takes sustained effort to raise carbon levels. If you are considering improving production methods you are better off doing so now as you will receive an increased return from productivity increases as a result of those practices in addition to any carbon credit revenue. Our advice is not to delay. |

| Do you have to sample at the same time of year each sampling round? | Yes you have to sample within 30 days either side of the baseline sample date. |

| If the use of the modelled carbon credits is shown to be an overestimate at the next physical sampling is repayment required? If so, how would that be paid back? | No, there is just a reduced issuance of credits at the physical sampling point. |

Question

Response points

We have had a couple of very good seasons and so carbon levels will be high at the moment, why should we not wait to baseline in a drought when it will drop again?

Carbon levels will certainly fluctuate with rainfall as it drives the production of carbon in the system and microbial populations.

It takes sustained effort to raise carbon levels.

If you are considering improving production methods you are better off doing so now as you will receive an increased return from productivity increases as a result of those practices in addition to any carbon credit revenue.

Our advice is not to delay.

When soil sampling what data apart from carbon is provided, or can be provided?

As standard we only test what is needed for the reporting. However, you can add and pay for other tests.

Provide the client with the service price list from EAL so they understand what can be provided.

Could you detail how the option of Agriprove to cover baselining costs is paid back?

We quote a cash value and a value in ACCUs but the ACCU price is discounted because it is tied to an existing contract.

Current price is set at $8.90.

Do you have to sample at the same time of year each sampling round?

Yes you have to sample within 30 days either side of the baseline sample date.

If the use of the modelled carbon credits is shown to be an overestimate at the next physical sampling is repayment required? If so, how would that be paid back?

No, there is just a reduced issuance of credits at the physical sampling point.
