## What made Bill effective — the headline read

Bill's success was **not** in raw closing technique. It was in three compounding habits that turned the role of "salesman" into "trusted technical expert who also happens to sell":

1. **Multi-method literacy as positioning.** [high] Bill could converse fluently across soil carbon, Reef Credits (Grazing Land Management), Tags-for-Tonnes (T4T) bundling, and Future Feeds / Skye demonstration partnerships. He didn't pitch one product to every prospect — he diagnosed which methodology fit and led with that. The artifact that recurs most often is him explaining adjacent methodologies *to landholders* in plain English with practical next steps.
2. **Long-form discovery → substantive deliverable.** [high] He invested in hour-long discovery calls (his own words: *"fantastic hour-long chat"* re: Tom Webster, 6,000ha Bathurst) and followed up with custom deliverables — Carbon Ready Reports, info decks, scoping-study responses. The Pardoo Beef Corporation scoping study (April 2024) is the strongest single example: AgriProve's formal response to a major corporate prospect was authored under Bill.
3. **Network-as-asset, including post-departure.** [high] He continued routing landholders to AgriProve **after he left for Ceres Tag** — Rod (June 2025), Monica @ Pardoo Wagyu (Aug 2025), Mark (May 2025 inbound). His personal network became durable infrastructure for AP's funnel.

If a current rep wanted to replicate Bill, the single biggest behavioural shift is probably **#1 — invest in methodology literacy outside your primary product line**. Most of the team sells soil carbon. Bill sold the *option space* (carbon, Reef Credits, T4T, partnerships) and let prospects self-select into what fit them best.

## Voice and style — verbatim patterns

These are exact phrasings from his sent emails. They give the team a starting point if anyone wants to write in Bill's voice (e.g. when reactivating one of his old contacts).

**Greetings and opens:**
- `"Hi [name],"` — universal
- `"I hope you are well and..."` — soft check-in before substance
- `"I trust your conversation with [name] the other day went well."` — references prior context, makes the email feel continuous
- `"Thanks for the flattering introduction, Dylan."` — accepts intros warmly, then transitions immediately to the prospect

**Conversational anchors:**
- `"Great question!"` — engagement starter (used regularly, not throwaway)
- `"It sounds like it would be worth a further conversation..."` — pivots interest into a meeting
- `"Got a really interested bloke here."` — internal handover voice (Aussie casual)
- `"Nice work!!"` — supports teammates by name

**Casual register (internal):**
- `"I'll get to it this arvo."` — Aussie idiom, conveys responsiveness without formality
- `"Cheers,"` — closes internal emails

**Formal close:**
- `"Kind regards, Bill Hyem | Key Account Manager"` — followed by full signature block
- Signature block consistently: `Role | Phone 1300 467 645 | Mobile 0428 431 732 | Email william@agriprove.io | Address: Brisbane, QLD`

**Self-deprecating fillers:**
- `"Please use this version. SharePoint is killing me."` — warm humility around process friction
