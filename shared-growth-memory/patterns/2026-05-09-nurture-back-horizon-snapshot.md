## Pattern

When a prospect closes lost with a timing objection ("not now", "6-12 months", "let me see how the market moves"), re-engaging 6-12 months later with a **fresh HORIZON Snapshot framed around what's changed in AgriProve over that period** has converted in both Ben's call-based outreach and Hobbs's on-farm follow-ups.

The re-engagement isn't a sales push — it's a "your numbers have changed" delivery. Two surface forms observed:

1. **Ben's call-based version (2026-04-24)** — frames the call around platform changes ("we've had so much change with their HORIZON"), with the snapshot follow-up as the artifact. The customer was "particularly receptive because we have had so much change."

2. **Hobbs's on-farm version (fv-2026-04-05)** — re-engages with the snapshot in hand, walks through what's changed in the platform: methodology rigour, refined LMS, updated ACCU revenue projections. Distillate notes the snapshot "showed materially better numbers vs prior year."

## How to apply

For a deal flagged as nurture-back-eligible (closed_lost ≥ 6 months ago with timing reason):

1. Generate a fresh HORIZON Snapshot for the property. Pull the **current** ACCU revenue projection — must be materially different from the snapshot they last saw.
2. Send with a "what's changed in 12 months" reframe. Don't lead with the ask; lead with the platform delta.
3. If positive response → escalate to Hobbs for an on-farm visit, snapshot in hand.

## What we don't yet know

- Conversion rate from re-engagement → on-farm visit → active pipeline. Working estimate ~40% based on Ben's anecdote, but n is too small to trust without a wider sample.
- Whether the pattern holds outside Riverina + Northern Tablelands (regional farming context might affect what "platform changes" land).
- Whether there's an optimal nurture window (6 months? 12? 18?). Ben's example was 12 months; we don't have data on shorter or longer cycles.
- Whether the approach works for nurtures that closed lost for non-timing reasons (e.g., "Insufficient commitment" — different objection structure).

These gaps should be filled by the next 90 days of data once the live pipeline is running.
