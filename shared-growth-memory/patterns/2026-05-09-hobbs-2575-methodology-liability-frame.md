## Pattern

The 25/75 revenue split is the clearest commercial objection in the loss data ("25%, too high" is the verbatim enum value). Reps who present it as **commission** ("we take 25%") get pushback. Reps who present it as **risk transfer** ("our 25% is what stops you carrying the methodology liability for 25 years") get acceptance.

Hobbs's specific phrasing has landed in three distinct visits across two NRM regions. The reframe accomplishes three things:
1. Makes the 25-year project length visible *as protection* rather than as a liability the customer carries
2. Surfaces methodology compliance as a hidden cost the customer doesn't have to think about
3. Implicitly distinguishes AgriProve from competitor models (DIY, fee-for-service)

The variant for DIY-preference customers — "You've got the agronomy. We've got the regulator." — does the same job by partitioning the work into "what you're already doing well" vs "what we add."

## How to apply

When a customer surfaces:
- "25% is too high" / "AgriProve's cut feels steep" / any commission-framed objection → **"Our 25% is what stops you carrying the methodology liability for 25 years."** Then: walk through what methodology liability means under the ERF (audit defence, baseline rigour, etc.).
- "Couldn't we do it ourselves?" / DIY framing → **"You've got the agronomy. We've got the regulator."** Then: distinguish ERF compliance + audit defence under the Clean Energy Regulator from in-farm agronomy work.

These framings should be **verbatim battle-card content** for reps. Don't paraphrase — the specific words are doing the work.

## What we don't yet know

- Whether either framing lands as effectively in cooler customers (3 observed instances were all customers Hobbs assessed as warming or already engaged). Cold customers might need different framing.
- Whether competitor-mention customers ("we're talking to X") respond to the same framing or need a more competitive comparison.
- Whether the "regulator" word lands in regions outside Riverina + Northern Tablelands. Some regions may have a more sceptical posture to regulator framing — flag for monitoring.
