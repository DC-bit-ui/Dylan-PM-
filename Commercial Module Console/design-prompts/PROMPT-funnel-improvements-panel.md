# The funnel improvements section

A new section for the console. It answers a question the reader will not ask out loud.

The module commits to assembling 125,000 hectares of grazing land, property by property, inside about two years. The reader is a carbon desk professional who has done the arithmetic and knows that is more land than has ever been signed in a comparable period. **What this section answers is: what changed in the machine that makes that credible.**

It is not a metrics wall and it is not a marketing panel. It is evidence, and its persuasive force comes entirely from being checkable.

---

## 1 · The improvements sit on the funnel, not beside it

The console already carries the recruitment funnel in hectares. **Each improvement attaches to the step of that funnel it acted on.** A reader should be able to see where capacity was added, and by direct consequence, where it was not.

The chain runs: unknown landholder → assessed and eligible → contacted → farm visit → strategy → commercial terms → signed.

Presenting these as a list beside the funnel loses the entire argument. Presenting them **on** the chain turns a set of claims into a shape: the machine got wider at the top and is unchanged in the middle. That shape is the finding.

**Steps where nothing has improved must remain visible and unmarked.** An empty step is not a gap in the story, it is the story. It tells the reader where the next investment goes, and a reader who can see the unimproved steps will believe the improved ones.

---

## 2 · Two registers, and the difference between them is load-bearing

**Register one: measured.** A before figure, an after figure, the window each was drawn over, and the population size.

**Register two: shipped, result pending.** A capability that is live but has not yet produced a measurable cohort. It carries what it replaces, the date it went live, and **the date it becomes measurable.**

These must be visually distinct without being labelled as strong and weak. A shipped capability with no result yet is not a lesser claim, it is a forward one, and for this reader it is often the more interesting of the two because it is the part still to arrive.

**Never present register two as register one.** A capability with no result presented as a result is the single failure that would cost the whole section its credibility, and it would take the rest of the console with it.

---

## 3 · Every entry carries its own provenance

Each entry states, as part of the entry rather than as a footnote:

- **What changed**, in plain terms
- **From what, to what**
- **The window each figure was drawn over**
- **How many observations sit behind it**, or the volume if a count is the metric

An entry that cannot supply all four does not appear.

This reader discounts unqualified figures automatically, and a stated sample size of fifteen reads as more honest than no sample size at all. **Disclosed uncertainty does not reduce trust in the source. Undisclosed uncertainty that the reader detects for themselves destroys it.**

## 4 · The entries

### Measured

**Assessment issuance rate** · top of funnel
One personalised soil carbon assessment issued every 23.6 hours, now one every 6.4 hours. Approximately 3.7 times. Reported 7 July 2026, measured over the preceding weeks. Marginal cost approximately two cents per assessment.

**Assessment conversion** · top of funnel
4 per cent of issued assessments convert to a prospect conversation, measured since launch.

**Field pipeline built** · contact to farm visit
1,500 landholder leads generated and 1,800 contactable landholders on file. 100,000 hectares booked into farm visits as at 29 May 2026, including 12 bookings in that week alone.

**Territory campaign throughput** · assessed to contacted
The quarterly recruitment target was exceeded using 60 per cent of the time allotted to it.

**Self-service property intake** · unknown landholder to assessed
A landholder can draw their own property boundary and request an assessment with no account and no call. Live 25 June 2026, running at approximately five submissions per day as at 2 July 2026.

**Commercial terms production** · strategy to terms issued
Median 60.8 days to produce and issue commercial terms in the first half of 2025, over 15 deals. Median 30.9 days in the first half of 2026, over 15 deals. Approximately half.

**Whole commercial runway** · qualified to terms issued
Median 74.4 days from a qualified landholder to issued commercial terms in the first half of 2025, over 14 deals. Median 66.3 days in the first half of 2026, over 15 deals. Approximately 11 per cent.

### Shipped, result pending

**Mapping and commercial terms automation** · strategy to terms issued
The spatial mapping that sits behind a commercial terms package was assembled by hand in desktop GIS software. It is now generated in the platform, with exclusion zones derived automatically. Live from July 2026. **First measurable cohort completes in Q4 2026.**

**In-call map confirmation** · farm visit to strategy
Confirming a property boundary with a landholder required sending a PDF and waiting for it to come back, a process that ran into weeks. It is being replaced by a link the landholder opens on their phone during the call. **Baseline recorded, result not yet instrumented.**

## 5 · The two commercial-terms entries belong side by side

The step improved by about half. The whole runway improved by about a tenth. **Both are true, they are drawn from the same deals, and they must appear together.**

The gap between them is the most valuable thing on the panel. It says the constraint moved rather than lifted, and it says the business knows exactly where its constraint now sits. A reader who is handed only the larger number and later works out the smaller one will discount everything else on the screen. A reader who is handed both reads a company that measures itself honestly, which is the entire proposition being sold here.

Do not soften this, annotate it, or explain it away. Place the two figures adjacent and let the reader draw the conclusion. It is not a weakness in the section, it is the section's proof of good faith.

---

## 6 · Form

**The magnitude of each change must be readable as a shape before any number is read.** Every entry is a movement from one value to another on a single quantity, and the size of that movement is the content.

The hard constraint: these changes span two orders of magnitude. One is a 3.7 times increase, another is an 11 per cent reduction, and some are counts that had no prior value at all because the capability did not exist. **A single linear scale renders the small changes invisible and makes the panel a chart of one entry.** The panel needs a treatment where an 11 per cent change and a 3.7 times change are both legible and honestly comparable.

Improvements in different directions must not be confusable. Some quantities are better when larger, some when smaller. **Direction of benefit is carried by the mark, never by colour alone**, and never by an arrow that means "up" in one row and "good" in another.

Entries that have no prior value are a distinct case, not a change from zero. A capability that did not exist is not an infinite improvement, and drawing it as one is the kind of thing that gets a section dismissed.

No explanatory copy. Values, units, windows, dates and sample sizes are labels and they stay. Anything that explains what a metric means or why it matters comes out.

---

## 7 · What does not appear

- **Any figure without a stated window and population.**
- **Any claim of improved conversion or win rate.** Conversion has not improved and the section must not imply it has, by omission, adjacency or arrangement.
- **Any number attributing a cycle-time result to a capability that shipped after the measurement window.**
- **Any dollar value**, on a credit volume or on an internal cost saving. Internal cost savings are not the reader's concern and reading about them makes the section about the wrong party.
- **Any figure that cannot be traced to a dated source.**

---

## 8 · Placement and behaviour

This section sits below the recruitment position, not above it. It is supporting evidence for a claim made elsewhere, and it should never be the first thing read.

It is present at every time state, and **its entries change as the module ages**. Before the module opens it carries recruitment capability, because recruitment is the whole question. From first crediting it carries delivery capability alongside it: sampling throughput, model validation, issuance turnaround.

**Selecting an entry scopes the view to the funnel step it acts on**, so the reader can see the step it changed rather than being told about it.

An entry moving from pending to measured is a real event and belongs in the console's event record on the date it happens.

---

## 9 · Constraints

- Australian English. **No em dashes anywhere, including titles.** Spaced hyphens instead.
- Nothing modelled is presented as measured or validated.
- Empty states carry their reason and the date they resolve.
- Unconsented landholders are never identifiable.
- Nothing moves position between time states.

## What I am not specifying

Proportion, grid, type, colour values, motion, or the mark used for a change. Those are the work. The requirements are that magnitude arrives before reading does, that changes two orders of magnitude apart are both legible on one panel, and that a step with no improvement is as visible as a step with one.
