# Remix B: the same console, with less in it

One of two parallel remixes being made from the current build. **This one is deliberately conservative.** Keep the structure, the sections and the visual approach as they are. Apply the removals, simplifications and label changes below, and add nothing that is not on this list.

A second remix is being made in a different direction. Do not try to anticipate or incorporate it here. The point of this version is to be the same console, cleaner and shorter, and to be finished quickly.

---

## Remove entirely

**The recruitment engine view.** Stage dwell times, weekly registration rates against internal targets, and the recruitment source breakdown. The fill projection view stays.

**"What changed in the way we work".** The before-and-after operational rows and the ACCU delta beneath them.

**The spot against locked price chart.**

**"What moved the model yields".** It repeats the model monitoring panel above it.

**The recruitment-closed summary on the delivery tab.**

**The reversal buffer row** in the validation figures.

**The personalisation tab.** Its function moves into the conversational surface at the foot of the console.

**The early-harvest interaction.** The crediting method locks a cohort to an annual reporting cadence, so harvest timing is not something the reader can move. In its place, show the scheduled estimation or validation event date, at headline size, in the empty space above where the control currently sits.

Anything removed should be removed completely, including any state or styling it left behind. It should not be reachable by scrolling, resizing, or moving between time states.

---

## Move

**Climate and land condition** moves from the fulfilment tab to the delivery tab. It concerns the land producing credits, not the land being recruited.

---

## Simplify

**Lead the fulfilment tab with progress against target as a percentage.** Pipeline depth stays, subordinate to it.

**Region cards** keep their shape thumbnail and lose everything beneath it - the percentage and hectare detail, the opening date, the instrument tags, the field team count, and the aggregate contracted line. Name, shape, one figure. The detail appears on selection.

**Pipeline stages** lose all their explanatory subtext. Stage one becomes "Horizon assessed and eligible". Stages four and five currently overlap, one describing commercial terms going out and the next a contract being issued - reword so they are distinct steps.

**Cohorts are drawn as coloured dots**, not polygons and not joined by lines. Colour the project dots by cohort. Cohort membership is dispersed and cohorts overlap each other spatially, so any drawn boundary implies territory a cohort does not hold. The cohort cards themselves stay as they are.

**The property list** shows five rows with an internal scroll behind them, sortable by land size, projected return, or ACCUs per hectare per year. On the delivery tab it sorts by volume delivered to date.

**Strip explanatory copy throughout.** Subtext under headings, footnotes under charts, captions under figures. Where the title and the chart are clear, none of it is needed. Values, units, dates and labels stay.

**Reduce the visual system.** Three type sizes at most. Fewer colours, fewer shades, fewer weights. This is currently the biggest single drag on how the console reads.

---

## Rename and relabel

- "Ask HORIZON" becomes **Horizon AI**, with a prompt along the lines of *How can I help you today?* rather than an instruction to ask about the module. Add an attachment control so a document can be brought into the conversation.
- "Nameplate" becomes **at capacity** throughout.
- "All land physically sampled" becomes **reference baseline established across all properties**.
- Every credit volume is labelled as **the purchaser's share**, net of the landholder share and the reversal discount. That is already the number being shown; the label needs to say so wherever it appears.

---

## Add, and only these

**Total addressable market per catchment**, with a penetration figure against it showing pipeline and signed land as a share of the grazing and pasture land the catchment actually holds.

**Additional harvestable volume from outside the module**, as a section below the reader's own cohorts. An expression of interest rather than a transaction - registering a bid on volume available from projects outside their module.

**A disable path** for any capability that has been enabled, reachable from where it was enabled.

---

## Constraints

- Australian English, and no em dashes anywhere including titles. Spaced hyphens instead.
- Three type sizes at most.
- Modelled figures described as modelled.
- Landholders who have not consented are never individually identifiable.

Nothing else changes. If something on the list would be better solved another way, note it rather than doing it, because this version's value is in being a predictable subtraction from what already exists.
