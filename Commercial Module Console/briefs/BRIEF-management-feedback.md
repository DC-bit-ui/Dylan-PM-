# Console revisions from the management review

Nineteen changes across the console. **Fifteen of them remove something.** Two change the geography the map is built on, and those two are dealt with first because everything else sits inside them.

The principle throughout stays as it was: **visual-led, with as little copy as possible.** Meaning in shape, weight and fill. Words for a name or a value only.

---

## 1 · Deployment zones replace catchments as the operational unit

The zones the module is recruited from are not catchments. They are **deployment zones: the area a field agent can cover on a two to three hour drive from their base.**

**Draw them as hexagons.** A hexagon is honest about what the thing is - a service radius around a person rather than a natural boundary - and hexagons tile without gaps, so adding a zone is a clean visual act rather than a new irregular blob.

Sizing, so they do not come out arbitrary. At a two and a half hour drive on rural roads, allowing for road distance against straight line, a zone works out around **160 kilometres from centre to vertex, or roughly 6.6 million hectares.** That sits between the two southern catchments already mapped, so it is the right order of magnitude. A tighter two hour zone is about 4.3 million hectares and a wider three hour zone about 9.6 million.

Each hexagon centres on the town its field agent works from. **Rename the section to Deployment Zones**, and remove the "Opens" date from the cards.

### Catchments do not disappear, they change job

This matters and it is worth getting right rather than discovering later.

**Deployment zones are where the work happens. Catchments are what the land can earn.** Water instruments are accounted by catchment - reef sediment credits apply to reef-draining catchments and nothing else - so eligibility remains a catchment property and cannot be attached to a hexagon that may straddle two of them.

So the two geographies coexist, with different jobs:

- **Hexagonal deployment zones** are the operational foreground. Where field agents are, where recruitment happens, what the module fills from.
- **Catchments** become the opportunity layer beneath, surfaced by the soil carbon and water lenses. That is already what they do.

A zone therefore carries the instruments of whichever catchments it overlaps, rather than owning one.

## 2 · Fulfilment becomes the lead metric, and the headline is a percentage

Recut the fulfilment projection to focus on the 125,000 hectare target, and make it the first thing on the surface.

**Lead with per cent of module filled, not a multiple.** A multiple of pipeline against target answers a question nobody asked first.

The progression to show, in order:

**Total opportunity → Qualified → Signed to date**

That reads as a funnel narrowing to a commitment, which is the shape of the argument. "Total opportunity" replaces "assessed and eligible" as the outward-facing name.

## 3 · The pipeline simplifies to four stages

Four categories, matching the language already used in the deck:

**Total opportunity · Qualified · Onboarding · Contracted**

Everything currently between those collapses into them. Fewer stages, no subtext under any of them.

## 4 · Remove entirely

**The HORIZON READ panel.** Both instances, on the fulfilment view and on delivery. Gone completely rather than moved or shrunk.

**Banked volume, and all surrender language.** We have no visibility on what the buyer banks or surrenders - only on what we deliver. Anything that implies otherwise is an assumption about their internal treatment that we cannot support.

**Harvestable volume available outside the module.** On checking, the additional volume in question is surplus delivered above the scheduled amount *within* the module, which is already visible in delivery against target. The separate element was double-counting.

**Cost on the cohort cards.** No need to show it.

**The duplicated validation date** on the cohort cards.

**The connector lines between cohort members.** Dots only.

**Land condition.** Out of delivery for version one. If it is kept anywhere it belongs in the marketplace, as a capability rather than a standing panel.

**ACCU capacity from the land performance list.** Show estimated ACCUs to date and nothing else.

**All explanatory text from the recruitment curve.** The chart itself is working.

## 5 · Delivery simplifies

The position-by-compliance-year view reads as confusing. **Reduce it to two things:**

- **The module delivery schedule**
- **Delivery against target, year on year**

Drop the compliance framing, the tranche comparison and the surrender vocabulary. We are describing what we deliver and when, against what was contracted. Nothing about how the buyer uses it.

If there is a better way to show delivery against contract than a schedule and a year-on-year comparison, it is worth trying - but simpler than what is there now, not more elaborate.

## 6 · Reconciliation

Rename the validation section to **Reconciliation**, or similar.

**Simplify to model against actual, and the variance between them, rolled up across all crediting cohorts.** Drop the phrase "sampled tenth" - that is internal method language and it makes the reader work.

This keeps the useful part, which is a track record of how close the model has been, without the vocabulary.

## Two consequences worth naming

**With HORIZON READ removed from both views, and Horizon AI possibly moving to the top bar, the intelligence layer becomes almost invisible on the surface.** The claim that this is a living system rather than a reporting dashboard currently rests on those two elements. Removing one and relocating the other is defensible, but the claim needs somewhere else to live - most likely inside the reconciliation track record and the model movement, which are evidence of a model reading land continuously rather than assertions about it.

**Hexagons and catchments must not be conflated.** A zone is an operating radius; a catchment is an accounting boundary for water instruments. If a hexagon is ever labelled with a catchment name, or a catchment is ever drawn as a hexagon, the distinction is lost and eligibility becomes unexplainable.

## Constraints

- Australian English, no em dashes anywhere including titles, spaced hyphens instead.
- Three type sizes at most, one shared colour and weight set. This pass should end with less on screen than it started with.
- No explanatory copy. Names, values and units only.
- Modelled figures described as modelled.
- All volumes are the purchaser's share.
- Landholders who have not consented are never individually identifiable.
