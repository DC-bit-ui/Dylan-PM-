# Regions of focus cards: the real data

The three catchment cards can now carry researched figures. This is the card behaviour and the data to put in it.

---

## Card behaviour

**Before it is selected**, a card carries three things: the catchment name, its shape, and contracted hectares against that region's target contribution. Nothing else.

**On selection**, it opens the same six fields on every card, in the same order, so a reader comparing catchments never has to work out which figure they are looking at.

The six fields:

1. Catchment area
2. Grazing land
3. Grazing enterprises
4. Holdings over 100 hectares
5. Agricultural output
6. Assessed and eligible, and what that is as a share of grazing land

Three of the eighteen cells are not yet derived. **Those cells still appear, carrying what they will hold and who derives it**, in the same way empty states elsewhere in the console carry their reason. A missing figure in a consistent grid reads as work in progress. A missing field breaks the grid and makes the reader hunt.

## The figure that does the most work

Penetration. **The module needs 125,000 hectares and the three catchments hold over 31 million between them**, of which more than 15 million is grazing land in the two catchments where a grazing share is published.

Current pipeline across all three is 355,200 hectares, which is around one per cent of the total area and under three per cent of grazing land anywhere.

That low number is the point. It converts the recruitment story from "we have signed this much" into "here is the size of the ground we work and how far into it we are", which is a far better answer to whether the target is reachable. Give it prominence over the raw hectare counts.

## Provenance the cards should carry

One line, once, rather than a footnote per figure:

> Agricultural figures from Australian Bureau of Statistics agricultural census data via New South Wales and Queensland government regional profiles. Catchment areas from state water agencies and the Bureau of Meteorology.

Two things worth knowing while placing the figures:

**The New South Wales agricultural data is 2015-16.** That is the last complete regional picture, because the most recent Bureau of Statistics release reported that survey response quality was too low to produce a full set of regional statistics. Sound on direction, a decade old on the levels.

**The Lachlan figures are drawn from sub-regions that overlap the catchment rather than match it**, and they include some country that drains elsewhere. Treat them as an upper bound.

---

## Two accuracy points

**Fitzroy has two published areas.** 142,544 km2 is the hydrological catchment; 156,000 km2 is the wider natural resource management region including adjacent coastal basins. Use 142,544 km2 - it is the more conservative and it is the one the water accounts use.

**The Murrumbidgee card and Mulloon.** Mulloon Creek, near Bungendore, drains east toward the Shoalhaven rather than west into the Murrumbidgee. Worth checking which catchment that property belongs in before it sits on a card, because water instrument eligibility is inherited from the catchment and a named property in the wrong one is the sort of thing a technically literate reader finds first.

## Constraints

- Australian English, no em dashes anywhere including titles, spaced hyphens instead.
- Three type sizes at most, and the same colour and weight treatment on all three cards.
- No explanatory sentences on the cards. Values, units and field labels only.
- Any figure marked as still to be derived says so plainly rather than showing a zero or a dash.
