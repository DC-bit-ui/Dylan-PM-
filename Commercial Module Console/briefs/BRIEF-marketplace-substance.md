## The finding that changes items 1, 2 and 3

From the BHP dossier, section 5. **BHP FY2026 is its first mandatory AASB S2 report. FY2027 brings limited assurance over everything including Scope 3. FY2029 brings reasonable assurance over everything, with the modified liability shield expired.**

Reasonable assurance means **the auditor re-performs the calculation.** Not reviews it. Re-performs it.

So the forcing function is not reporting and it is not cost. The dossier says this outright: *"The forcing function is assurance, not cost."* Their FY2024-25 ACCU bill was around A$17m on an equity basis, which is nothing to them.

That reframes the whole capability. **ESG and climate reporting is not a reporting product. It is an evidence chain that survives re-performance.** And it reframes what the environment looks like, because a framework list with progress bars is a status report, and a carbon desk already has those.

## 1 and 2 · ESG and climate reporting

`esg-environment.png`.

### The card

The front door should show the thing the environment does, which is hold the chain. Not four coverage bars. The current preview is honest and it is the least differentiated of the five.

### The environment, three parts

**The assurance clock as the hero.** FY2026, FY2027, FY2029, with the bar rising three times in four years and a marker on where they are now. Nothing else on the screen creates urgency the way a dated statutory escalation does, and it is theirs rather than ours.

**The chain of custody for one number.** Georeferenced sample point, NATA accredited laboratory with certificate and method code, gazetted method determination and version, calculation engine build and parameter set, aggregated figure with its boundary, and then the handover. Every link openable.

This is the product. From the dossier, section 5.2, what third-party land data must clear:

- a **prescribed method, not a bespoke one**, because AASB S2 para 29 requires the measurement approach, inputs and assumptions to be disclosed and a proprietary model with no published method has nowhere to sit
- **method hierarchy and site-specificity**, because the auditor will ask which tier applies and why a lower tier was acceptable
- **traceability from field to reported figure**, with every transformation reproducible from retained records

Nobody else can show that chain, because nobody else holds every link. It is the difference between a number and an auditable number.

**The companion builds the pack.** Not a chatbot that explains standards. An agent that assembles the artefact: sampling register, laboratory certificates, method citation per cohort, calculation trace, figure with boundary. And then, unprompted, **the gap list**: what it could not evidence and why.

The gap list is the most valuable panel on the screen, because it is the only one that generates the next conversation.

### The hard constraint

Dossier, line 473: **contributing inputs only. Never imply we produce their report.** The last link in the chain is a handover, not an output. This one matters legally as well as commercially, and it should be visible in the interface rather than only in our heads.

## 3 · Mine rehabilitation, and this is the strongest story we have

### Why it works

Queensland's grazing post-mining land use specification requires **landform stability and erosion comparable to surrounding non-mined landforms.** The dossier puts it plainly: *"the analogue-site problem and the neighbour-comparison problem are the same problem."* **That is DROVER, by another name.** DROVER already benchmarks a property against least-disturbed areas within 10 km.

We are not adapting a tool to a new problem. The regulator wrote the criterion in DROVER's own terms.

### The evidence gap, in their words

Mt Arthur FY2025 Annual Rehabilitation Report: **5,940.93 ha disturbed, zero hectares achieving rehabilitation completion.** The stated reason is that *"vegetation has not matured to a stage where it can be demonstrated that it will be sustainable for the long term."* Monitoring is manual transects plus remote sensing used for erosion only.

BMA has more than 40,000 ha requiring rehabilitation at a **7% completion rate, the lowest among Queensland coal peers** against a sector range of 7 to 52%.

### The demo, and it should be one property

A pastoral enterprise adjacent to a mining lease where DROVER has already run. One frame carrying three things:

**The reference distribution.** Least-disturbed neighbours within 10 km, drawn as a distribution rather than a single comparison, because a distribution is what "comparable to surrounding non-mined landforms" actually means.

**The rehabilitation area against it.** Cover and erosion, as delta from baseline. Never soil carbon change, per the standing position and the Judo findings.

**The trajectory, and this is the whole pitch.** BHP cannot currently say when a hectare will meet criteria. Their own report says it cannot yet be demonstrated. A continuous measured trajectory against a reference distribution lets you **put a date on it, with an interval.** That is the thing they do not have and cannot get from transects.

### Two honesty positions, and they are in the dossier for a reason

We have no track record in mining rehabilitation, so the first engagement is a **validation against their existing field survey, not a replacement for it.** And whether a state regulator would accept satellite-derived evidence against completion criteria is untested by us. The initial value is continuity and spatial completeness between surveys.

### The CFO hook, flagged and not yet usable

If continuous evidence supports earlier demonstration against completion criteria, it may affect when a provision can be released. BHP's closure provision is US$10.5bn and a one-year acceleration of cash flows moves it by **US$291m**. The dossier marks this unverified and says to test it with someone who knows the accounting and state regulatory treatment before saying it out loud. I would keep it off the console entirely until that is done.

## 4 · The fulfilment bar

`bar-and-credits.png`, top half.

**The bar is telling the most pessimistic true story available.** Five per cent filled, ninety five per cent empty, reading as a module that is failing. The same four numbers say the opposite.

Eligible 13,409,709. Qualified 355,200. Target 125,000. Signed 5,990.

**There is 107 times more eligible land than the module needs and the qualified pipeline is already 2.8 times the target.** Supply is not the constraint. Conversion is. That is a confident position and the current bar hides it completely.

A fill bar answers how full, and nobody is worried about how full at month one. Draw the supply cascade instead and the target line sits a long way inside the available pool. It needs a log scale, because 13.4 million against 125,000 cannot be drawn linearly.

The tech-enabled read you are after is free here: the cascade collapsing from eligible down to signed on load.

## 5 · Credits

`bar-and-credits.png`, bottom half.

**It became a document, and that is my fault.** The brief was analytically right and it produced prose. *1.3 times the unretired float* is a sentence. Two masses at true scale is a picture, and scarcity is a physical idea that wants mass rather than sentences.

Twelve blocks against sixteen blocks, one block per two thousand credits. The reader sees it before they read anything.

**First refusal is the best thing on the tab and Claude Design invented it.** Costs nothing, commits nothing, holds the volume while terms are drawn. It is currently a button under a paragraph. It should be the surface.

**Cut the three numbered bands below the fold.** Market position, coincidence and the same hectare are the reasoning that got us here, not the product. They belong behind a single control, the way the framework rigour already sits behind one.
