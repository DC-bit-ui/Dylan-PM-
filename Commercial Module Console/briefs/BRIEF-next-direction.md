# Next pass on the module holder console

An edit to what you have built. It goes further than the last pass in two ways: one governing principle about density, and a correction to how the module and its recruitment territories actually relate.

---

## The one principle

**Simplicity is the requirement, not a preference.**

Everything below is subordinate to it. If a section can be built two ways and one of them is quieter, build the quiet one. If a figure needs a sentence to be understood, change the figure rather than adding the sentence. If two elements do similar work, keep one.

The reason is not taste. This console sits alongside a commercial proposition the buyer will judge on price per hectare, and its job is not to make the argument - it is to make the argument look safe. **A busy screen reads as compensating for something.** A quiet screen with four hard numbers on it reads as a business that knows exactly where it stands.

**This is not an instruction to make it plainer, and it must not produce a wireframe.** The principle is visual-led with as little copy as possible. Meaning is carried by shape, weight, position and colour, and words appear only where a value, a unit, a date or a name is needed. A view with four confident marks and eleven words on it should be more beautiful than the version with paragraphs, not less, because the space the copy occupied goes to the marks. Quiet in the sense that a well-designed instrument is quiet. Not sparse, not unfinished, not grey.

Concretely: three type sizes at most, one small fixed set of colours and weights defined once, no explanatory subtext under headings or captions under figures, and one dominant element per view. Where a reader has to decode before they can read, the design has failed. Every time a sentence is removed, the design gets a chance to say the same thing better - take it.

## The model correction

The console currently allocates the module across catchments, each with its own target contribution, summing to the module target. **That is not how modules and territories actually relate, and it will not survive the second module.**

**A recruitment territory is an operational zone.** It is a campaign area worked by a field team - roughly two people, one in the field and one central. It exists before any module and outlives it. It has its own capacity, its own pipeline and its own rate of production.

**A module draws from whichever zones are producing.** There is no fixed one to one allocation. A module fills from multiple zones, and one zone will eventually feed several modules.

What changes on screen:

- A zone no longer shows "52,000 of 52,000 hectares" against a target it owns. It shows **what it has contributed to this module**, and separately **its own capacity and pipeline**.
- Module fill is the **sum of contributions**, not the sum of per-zone targets being satisfied.
- The language shifts from region and catchment to **operational zone** or **campaign**. Catchment stays as the geographic descriptor, since that is what the boundary actually is, but it is not the organising unit of the module.

This is a better story as well as a truer one. It says the business runs a recruitment machine with territories, and modules are filled from its output. That scales; a module carved into three fixed regional quotas does not.

## Four zones, not three

Year one now runs **four operational zones rather than three**, to hold buffer against the 125,000 hectare target.

Currently modelled: Lachlan, Murrumbidgee, Fitzroy. A fourth is being chosen.

**Burdekin is the strongest candidate** and worth building against unless told otherwise. It is Australia's third largest cattle region at roughly 1.3 million head, it is a Great Barrier Reef catchment so it carries Reef Credits eligibility as Fitzroy does, and it gives the module two reef-eligible zones rather than one. Confirm before the figures are treated as final.

Each additional zone carries two headcount, one field and one central. That ratio is worth having available somewhere, because it is the honest answer to how the machine scales.

## Map corrections

**Catchment boundaries are too loose and in at least one case extend into the ocean.** Use actual catchment boundaries clipped to the coastline. A polygon covering water in front of a technical audience costs more credibility than the whole rest of the map earns.

If true catchment geometry is not available, **postcode boundaries are an acceptable substitute** and are cleaner than an approximated shape. Either is defensible. An unbounded blob is not.

## Regulatory standing, which is currently missing entirely

A carbon buyer's largest unpriced risk is that the method changes under them. The console says nothing about this and it now can.

The method review has closed. **Five of six offsets integrity standards were met, the three times cap was accepted, Schedule 2 is intact, and the three tonnes per hectare commerciality is preserved with no further review until sunset.** Separately, **33 of this business's projects were scrutinised in that review and no systemic errors were found.**

That last fact is the strongest single sentence available to this product and it is not on the screen anywhere. It belongs where the reader will meet it early, stated plainly with its date, without adjectives.

## One set of funnel numbers

The funnel currently carries figures from more than one source and they do not reconcile with each other. Define each stage once, and let every number on the console derive from that single definition.

The measured conversion rates:

- **Qualified to onboarding: 50 per cent**
- **Onboarding to signed: 40 per cent**
- Close rate on commercial terms issued since 1 January: **50 per cent**

Which means signing 125,000 hectares requires roughly **312,500 hectares at onboarding** and **625,000 hectares qualified**. Assessed and eligible land currently stands at about 1.56 million hectares, which is around two and a half times the qualified requirement.

**That arithmetic is the console's credibility.** If any figure on screen cannot be reconciled to it, the figure is wrong or the stage is misnamed. Live opportunity currently in play is 64,127 hectares, which is a different quantity from assessed and eligible and needs a different label so the two are never read as the same thing.

The four year trajectory, if a longer horizon is useful anywhere: **125,000 hectares cumulative in year one, 419,000 in year two, 839,000 in year three, 1.25 million in year four.**

## Delivery leads forward

The delivery view currently opens on a result for one cohort. The reader's live question is whether they will get what they contracted.

**Lead with position over time.** For each period: what was committed, what has been delivered, what is modelled ahead, and where that leaves them. Delivered volume accumulates, modelled volume revises, and the gap between the two is the answer.

Position anchors that view for a structural reason rather than a preference - it is the only element meaningful at every point in the module's life, because the commitment and the schedule exist before any hectare does.

Beneath it, the cohorts as a **schedule of dated delivery events** rather than a list. Each carries its scheduled estimation or validation date and whether it is tracking to it.

Two figures carry the validation story. Per cohort a **waterfall**: modelled at estimation, the adjustment the sampled tenth made, what issued. Across the portfolio a **calibration plot**: every sampled cohort as one point, modelled against validated, the identity line through it. Points sitting on that line is the most persuasive image this product can produce, and a technical reader takes it in without a legend. At one cohort it is a single point and visibly so, which is honest.

Every modelled figure carries its **trajectory**, because the model re-reads this land continuously. When the portfolio number moves, say what moved it - a rise of four per cent, of which three points from above-median autumn rainfall in one zone. No other carbon product can tell a buyer why their forecast changed.

## Marketplace, grouped by whose land

Reduce to five capabilities of material weight, grouped not by product category but by **whose land they read**:

| Capability | Whose land |
|---|---|
| **ESG reporting agent** - obligations across every framework, evidence in hand, what is missing, drafted output | all of it |
| **Ecological co-benefits** - what else this ground generates, quantified on their own properties | land they hold credits on |
| **Mine rehabilitation monitoring** - ground cover, erosion and soil carbon read across post-mining landforms | land they operate |
| **Acquisition intelligence** - land condition and monetisable potential on ground before they bid | land they do not own yet |
| **Property potential** - which of their own holdings are worth registering | land they own that is not working |

They bought this pointed at other people's grazing country, and it reads any land. Three of the five concern their own estate, which for a large miner is a much bigger surface than the module.

The reporting agent is the destination rather than a peer - everything else produces measurement, and the agent turns it into filed disclosure. **One-click reporting is the stated goal, so build toward it rather than toward a document library.**

**The cards invert so the finding leads.** *1.23 million hectares of your acquisition pipeline already screened* opens far harder than *Acquisition screening*. The finding is proof the work is already done on their ground, and proof belongs before the pitch. **Preview on my land becomes the primary action**, with enabling following it.

When a new instrument becomes available on land they already hold, that arrives as the derived insight at the top of the view, not as a permanent banner. Rarely, dismissible, and staying dismissed.

## Horizon AI

The conversational surface reasons across the whole console and returns an answer with the relevant chart beside it rather than a block of text. It takes attachments.

Its personalisation scope stays narrow and deliberate: it records the reader's own commitments and reflects them against the delivery position, offering to mark a stated target on the charts as a reference line. It adds their context to what is shown; it does not change what is shown. Everything reversible.

Surface it early rather than at the end.

---

## Getting back out of a property

A property opens from its point on the map and its row in the list. Returning is a visible control always on screen, and **it returns the reader to exactly where they left** - same view, same time state, same scroll position, with the property still identifiable. A named path reads better than a back arrow because it says both where you are and where you land, and it keeps the module present while the reader is inside one farm.

It has to work from a shared link on someone else's machine. A demonstration is run by someone who did not build it.

## Constraints

- Australian English, no em dashes anywhere including titles, spaced hyphens instead.
- Three type sizes at most, one shared colour and weight set.
- Modelled figures described as modelled, never as measured or validated.
- All credit volumes are the purchaser's share, net of discount, labelled as such.
- No commentary on market price.
- Landholders who have not consented are never individually identifiable.
