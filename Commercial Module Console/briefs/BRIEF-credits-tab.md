# The Credits tab

Capabilities landed well. The function previews are doing what they were meant to do, and the mining rehabilitation and land acquisition cards in particular now show the product rather than describing it.

Credits did not land. It kept the card form and added paint: a coloured dot, coloured framework tags, and one explanatory sentence per card. The volume is still the headline and the frameworks are still decoration. It also introduced three new hues including a magenta that does not exist anywhere else in the console.

That is worth naming rather than glossing, because the interesting part is **why** it did not land. The change list said lead with the obligation rather than the volume, and the tab could not do that, because **the obligations it was pointing at do not hold up.** You cannot lead with an argument that is not true. So it did the only thing available and decorated the cards instead.

## The audit. Seven claims on that tab that do not stand up

This is the part you asked for, and it is worse than I expected.

| On the tab now | Verdict | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **TCFD**, on Biodiversity Certificates | Remove | The TCFD was **disbanded in 2023** and its monitoring responsibilities transferred to the ISSB from 2024. It is also a climate framework sitting on a nature instrument, so it is wrong twice over. |
| **Climate Active**, on Environmental Plantings ACCUs | Remove | The Australian Government has decided to **close the programme.** Consultation paper issued July 2026, a formal decision expected before the end of 2026, then a 12 or 18 month transition after which all licence agreements end. |
| **GRI 304**, on Reef Credits and Biodiversity Certificates | Replace | Superseded by **GRI 101: Biodiversity 2024**, effective 1 January 2026. GRI 304 is last year's standard. |
| **Scope 1**, on Environmental Plantings ACCUs | Remove | An offset is not a Scope 1 reduction. Scope 1 is direct emissions from owned or controlled sources. Surrendering an ACCU discharges a Safeguard obligation; it does not move a Scope 1 number. A carbon desk professional catches this in about two seconds. |
| **Scope 3**, on Reef Credits | Remove | A water quality credit has no bearing on Scope 3 emissions at all. |
| **Reef 2050**, on Reef Credits | Remove | It is a Commonwealth and Queensland water quality policy plan with catchment targets. It is not a corporate reporting standard and companies do not report against it. |
| **"The single largest gap in your TNFD submission, at 8 of 14 unanswered"** | Remove | We cannot see their submission. This is the riskiest sentence on the console: specific, checkable, and wrong the moment anyone checks. |

Four of the seven name a framework that has been retired, superseded, or is being wound up. Two are category errors. One asserts a fact about the buyer we have no access to.

**Some of this is mine.** My brief supplied illustrative counts and the phrase *closes 3 of 14*, which is the shape it built to. The instruction to build the form and leave the claim provisional was in the change list and it was the item most likely to be skipped, because a placeholder looks unfinished and a specific number looks done.

## What replaces it is stronger, not weaker

There is a mandatory Australian disclosure requirement that asks about carbon credits by name, and nobody has put it on the tab.

**AASB S2, paragraph 36(e).** Part of the Australian Sustainability Reporting Standards. Mandatory, in force, and BHP reports under it. Where an entity's net emissions target relies on carbon credits, 36(e) requires it to disclose four things:

| 36(e) asks for | Answerable from the instrument |
|---|---|
| the extent to which and how the target relies on credits | **No.** Theirs to state. |
| which third party scheme will verify or certify the credits | **Yes.** Clean Energy Regulator, ERF soil carbon method. |
| the type of credit, nature based or technological, and whether reduction or removal | **Yes.** Nature based, and a removal. |
| other factors bearing on credibility and integrity, including permanence assumptions | **Yes.** The permanence period on record for each project. |

Three of four, answerable from the product itself.

Why this beats the TNFD framing on every axis. It is **mandatory** rather than voluntary. It is **current** rather than superseded. It names **a paragraph** rather than an invented count. And every answer is a fact about our own instrument rather than a claim about their reporting, which means nothing on the tab depends on knowing something we cannot know.

## The structural correction, which is a correction of my own proposal

The stacked bar I sent last round was the wrong instrument, and the reason is worth having.

It put a **surrender obligation** and a **voluntary framework** on one axis with one unit. Those are not the same kind of thing. Surrendering an ACCU against a Safeguard excess emissions position is a legal act with exact arithmetic. Evidencing a TNFD disclosure is a judgement about whether some information supports a voluntary statement. A single bar implies they are commensurable, and they are not.

So it wants **three bands, each with its own unit and its own verb.**

| | Obligation | Unit | Verb | What reaches it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | Safeguard Mechanism | ACCUs | **discharges** | ACCUs only |
| **2** | AASB S2 para 36(e) | named requirements | **answers** | any credit, described properly |
| **3** | TNFD, GRI 101 | none, it is evidence | **evidences** | all five lines |

`credits-instrument.png` is that, drawn. Ignore the styling as always.

The three verbs are the whole discipline. Nothing on this tab should ever say a credit **closes** a disclosure.

## The honest device: ask for the one number we cannot know

Their Safeguard position is the figure that would make band 1 resolve, and we have no way to know it. The current tab's instinct would be to model it. Better to **leave the band open with an input.**

Three reasons it is the better design and not just the safer one. It removes the fabrication entirely. It turns the band into their arithmetic rather than ours, which is a stronger position to be in. And an open field is an invitation to engage, where an asserted number is something to either accept or dispute.

## The finding that reframes the whole tab

Adding it up: 220,000 ACCUs from the module and 9,600 from environmental plantings. **The additional ACCU line is 4% of the module**, and only two of the five lines reach the surrender band at all.

So **the tab does not sell more tonnes**, and it should stop trying. What it sells is coverage of questions the carbon module cannot answer: nature and water. That is a good argument, it is true, and it is completely different from the one the current headline makes.

The tab currently reads *Volume from land you already hold*, which points straight at the weakest version of the case. Volume is exactly where these lines are least impressive.

## Copy rules, so it cannot drift back

- **discharges** only for ACCU surrender. **answers** only for a named AASB S2 36(e) requirement. **evidences** for voluntary frameworks. Never **closes**.
- Never state a count, fraction or status of the buyer's own submission.
- Never tag an offset instrument with a Scope.
- Name a standard with its version and effective date, not just its acronym.
- Name the issuing entity on every line. Reef Credits and ACWIS water credits, Eco-Markets Australia. Soil carbon ACCUs, environmental plantings ACCUs and biodiversity certificates, the Clean Energy Regulator.
- No new hues. Olive for carbon instruments, blue for water instruments, lime for accent and marks. The magenta goes.

## Three things I have not verified, and you should before this ships

Being explicit rather than leaving them buried.

**The band 3 mapping is my assessment, not a sourced one.** Whether a soil carbon ACCU evidences a TNFD disclosure depends on which of the 14 you mean, and I have marked it at the framework level rather than the disclosure level. Defensible as drawn, not yet checkable.

**BHP's reporting group and period.** They are comfortably over the Group 1 thresholds, so AASB S2 applies, but the reporting period the console should reference is worth confirming rather than assuming.

**Whether the module's own ACCUs are available for the buyer's surrender.** This is a contract question rather than a design one, and it decides whether band 1 exists at all. If the 220,000 are committed to a purpose other than the buyer's own Safeguard position, the band's largest number comes out and the whole tab becomes bands 2 and 3.

## Constraints

- Australian English, and no em dashes anywhere including titles. Spaced hyphens instead.
- No price commentary.
- Modelled and forecast figures described as such, never as measured, validated or issued.
- The console reports outcomes, not process.
- Volumes are the purchaser's share.
- Visual led, with as little copy as possible. Three type sizes is the ceiling.
- Dark surface. `#7f9c18` olive and `#3987e5` blue are the validated area fills; `#cdfd29` lime is stroke, marker and low opacity fill only.
