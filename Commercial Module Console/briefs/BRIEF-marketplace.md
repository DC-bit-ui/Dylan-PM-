# The marketplace

Two tabs, Capabilities and Credits. Everything else on the console stays as it is.

The note on the cards is right, and it is worth being precise about why, because the fix follows from the diagnosis rather than from styling.

---

## The changes, so none of them get lost in the prose

Seven are settled and five are yours. The reasoning for each is below; this is the list so nothing has to be inferred from a paragraph.

**Settled**

1. Every capability card carries a **working miniature of its own surface**, populated with this land's data. Five capabilities, five different pictures.
2. The **finding comes out of caption type.** It is the card's argument, not its footnote.
3. The credits tab **leads with the obligation closed, not the volume available.**
4. **Solid means held, striped and unfilled means eligible.** The same grammar the map already uses for Reef Credits against ACWIS. No new colours.
5. Water Quality Credits **keeps its unquantified state, with a date.** No number on it.
6. **Enable is a transition from the miniature into the section**, not the appearance of something unseen.
7. The **0 enabled counter goes.** If the gap between what was found and what is being used is worth stating, it reads as a finding rather than a scoreboard.

**Yours to answer**

- The ordering principle on Capabilities. What puts one above another.
- Whether the credits instrument replaces the four cards or sits above them.
- Live miniatures or fixed renders.
- What a card becomes once it is enabled.
- Whether Capabilities and Credits are one surface or two.

**Build the form, not the claim**

The obligation counts and the *closes 3 of 14* phrasing are **provisional and not cleared.** Build the instrument with placeholder counts and a visible attribution slot for whatever standard the mapping cites. The reasoning is under *Two cautions* below and it matters more than anything else on this list.

## Why a card cannot sell this

**The finding is the sell, and it is set as caption text.** *1.23 million ha of your acquisition pipeline already screened.* That is an extraordinary sentence. It says we ran this on your ground, without being asked, and here is the number. It is currently the smallest type on the card, below the name and above the button.

**Five identical shapes means nothing is weighted.** A grid asks the reader to evaluate five things from scratch, in an order that carries no information. A marketplace where everything looks equally important sells nothing, because the reader's cheapest response is none of them.

**The card answers a question the reader is not asking.** It says what the capability is. They want to know what lands on their screen when they press the button. *Sediment and nutrient export measured across 11 catchments* is true and it still leaves them with no picture.

**One card is already doing the right thing and it is on the other tab.** Biodiversity Certificates says the finding is *the single largest gap in your TNFD submission, at 8 of 14 unanswered.* That is the only mark on either tab making an argument rather than a description, and it is an argument about a liability the reader already has. It is the model, not the exception.

---

## One push back on enticing, because it changes the design

The instinct to make these enticing is right and the word may lead somewhere weaker than the material deserves.

Everything on both tabs has **already been measured on their own ground.** Nothing here needs projecting or promising. So the strongest available posture is not enticement, it is **disclosure**: here is what we found on your land that you are not currently using. That is a harder sentence to argue with than any pitch, and it costs nothing, because it is true.

There is also a cost to the alternative that is easy to miss. This console's main asset is that a compliance professional trusts the Delivery tab. If the marketplace reads as advertising, it spends that trust on the tabs where it matters most. The two tabs sit one click apart.

The good news is that disclosure and enticement converge on the same design here. Proof is more enticing than persuasion when you already hold the proof.

## Capabilities: give each one a working miniature

The proposal in `marketplace-function-previews.png`.

**The preview is not a picture of the product. It is a small working instance of the product, populated with the buyer's own land.** That single move does four things at once, which is why it seems worth the space:

- It answers *what would this look like* with a picture instead of a sentence.
- It **is** the proof, because it is their data. The mark and the argument are the same object, so nothing has to be claimed twice.
- It differentiates the grid. Five capabilities produce five genuinely different pictures, so the tab stops reading as five rectangles and the reader's eye starts doing the sorting.
- It makes Enable a **continuous expansion of something already on screen** rather than the arrival of something unseen. That is the modern register, and it is the same principle that made the time stepper good.

The five sketched in the attachment, only to show how different they are from each other:

| | The picture |
|---|---|
| ESG and climate reporting | four frameworks as coverage, answerable against total |
| Catchment water accounting | eleven catchments shaded by export reduction |
| Mining rehabilitation monitoring | twenty four sites, eight years each, as a field of trajectories |
| Land acquisition screening | the national pipeline, eligible lit against screened out |
| Own land project registration | their own estate, the eligible fraction lit |

Ignore the styling entirely. The point is the variety and the fact that each one is a real instrument rather than an icon.

**Two things worth noticing about the attachment.** The mining card is the most persuasive of the five and it is the only one that shows time, which is probably not a coincidence. And the word doing the most work anywhere on the tab is **already** - *already screened*, *already generates*. It is doing more than the button is.

**`Preview on my land` was identified in the earlier scaffold review as the best interaction in the marketplace and it is currently absent.** If the card carries a live miniature, that interaction has somewhere to live.

Open, and genuinely: whether the miniature is live or a fixed render, and whether the preview **is** the card or the card opens into it. A live miniature is far more convincing and it is also five more surfaces to build.

## The counters are arguing against you

*5 capabilities on this land · 4 ready to enable now · 0 enabled on this module.*

The third number argues against the product. But the idea underneath is right: the ratio of what the twin found to what is being used is a **gap**, and a gap is what motivates. Worth finding the version that reads as a finding rather than a scoreboard of inaction.

---

## Is this a shop or a findings report

Worth deciding deliberately, because the form follows.

At five items there is no browsing problem to solve, and grids exist for browsing at scale. The headline already promises a findings report - *the twin reads land, carbon is only one use of it* - and then the form delivers a shop. **The headline is right and the form contradicts it.**

A shop is ordered by category. A findings report is ordered by consequence. Which raises the question the current tab does not answer: what is the order, and what would put one capability above another? Probably what the reader loses by not enabling it. Offered as the likely axis rather than the answer.

---

## Credits: the tags are the argument, and they are rendered as decoration

TNFD. GRI 304. Scope 3. Reef 2050. Nature Repair. Those tags mean something quite specific: **these credits answer reports the buyer is already legally obliged to file.** They are not shopping for credits. They are short of answers.

So the sentence is currently pointing the wrong way. *31,400 Reef Credits available to FY31* leads with volume. **Volume is our unit. Coverage of a report they must file is theirs.** Same discipline as the delivery schedule, where expressing delivery against a surrender year is closer to the question they answer to their regulator.

`marketplace-credits-obligation.png` is one way to turn the four cards into a single instrument. Obligations as rows, three states per row: answerable from the carbon module today, closable with a credit this land already generates, and no instrument at all.

**It needs no new colours, because the grammar already exists.** Solid means held. Striped and unfilled means eligible but not enabled. That is exactly what the map already says with Reef Credits against ACWIS, so the console ends up with one language for *you have this* against *you could have this*, learned once and used in two places.

**The selection is where it sells.** Choose Biodiversity Certificates and three obligation rows light up at once. The reader is no longer choosing a product, they are closing a gap, and the instrument does the arithmetic for them.

Two things this form gets for free that four cards cannot. A row that is **complete** is visible, which makes the incomplete rows credible. And an obligation with **no instrument at all** is visible, which is the honest version of a sales surface and the most likely thing to prompt a conversation.

### Two cautions on this one, both real

**The wording risk is precision.** Holding a biodiversity certificate does not itself answer a TNFD disclosure. It contributes evidence toward certain disclosures. *Closes 3 of 14* is the compelling phrasing and the overclaiming one, and the honest version needs finding rather than assuming. This is the strongest claim the console would make anywhere, so it is also the most expensive one to get wrong.

**The mapping is real work and it is not done.** Every obligation count in the attachment is illustrative. Framework to disclosure to instrument is a genuine piece of analysis, and the panel should probably be visibly attributable to whatever standard it maps against.

### The honest state is an asset, so keep it

Water Quality Credits reads *not yet quantified, pending catchment accounting.* The temptation will be to put a number on it. Do not. **A marketplace where one item admits it is not ready is what makes the other three believable**, and a Watching state with a date attached reads better than a bare absence.

---

## The enable moment is the product moment

Enabling a capability adds a section to the tab bar. That is the thing the demo turns on, and right now the section simply appears.

If the card carries a working miniature, then enabling can be a **continuous transformation** - the miniature travels to the tab bar and expands into the section - and the reader has already seen where they are going before they get there.

The related question nobody has asked yet: **what happens to the card once enabled?** Today it says Enabled and stays in the grid, which means the tab gets progressively less useful as the product succeeds.

---

## Not settled, and worth your view

Whether the credits instrument replaces the four cards or sits above them. The ordering principle on Capabilities. Live previews against fixed ones.

And the larger one: **whether Capabilities and Credits are two tabs at all.** They answer the same question - what else does this land do - in two registers, one about surfaces and one about instruments. It may be one surface with two kinds of finding on it.

---

## Constraints

- Australian English, and no em dashes anywhere including titles. Spaced hyphens instead.
- No price commentary anywhere on either tab.
- Modelled and forecast figures described as such, never as measured, validated or issued.
- Where a capability or credit line touches an actual instrument, name the entity that issues it. Reef Credits and ACWIS water credits, Eco-Markets Australia. Biodiversity certificates and environmental plantings ACCUs, the Clean Energy Regulator.
- The console reports outcomes, not process.
- Volumes are the purchaser's share.
- Visual led, with as little copy as possible. Three type sizes is the ceiling, and where the title and the mark are clear the explanatory sentence comes out.
- Dark surface. `#7f9c18` olive and `#3987e5` blue are the validated area fills; `#cdfd29` lime is stroke, marker and low opacity fill only.
