## What the brief asks for, in one page

**Six changes, in rough order of how much they matter.**

1. **Whole country always visible.** The frame no longer crops to the eastern seaboard.
2. **Full colour, undarkened by default.** The recession moves out of the resting state.
3. **The thematic lenses get their subject back.** Catchments come forward filled when their lens is selected, and the hexagons step back. Right now the lenses dim an already dim map and teach the reader nothing.
4. **Water splits into two lenses.** Reef Credits, 8 catchments, solid blue. ACWIS, 12 Murray Darling catchments, blue wash with stripes. Same colour, different fill state.
5. **Boots on the ground keeps the recession and the close crop.** That treatment was never wrong, it was wrong as a default. As the answer to "where can we reach" it is right.
6. **A new catchment lens.** All 219 boundaries, dashed and unfilled, hover to name and click to focus, no recession.

**One principle running through all of it:** a lens selects a subject and the subject is the brightest thing on screen, not the least dimmed. One lens, one treatment.

## Decisions embedded in the data, so you know what you have committed to

- **ACWIS is national, not reef restricted, which is why the water story got bigger.** Water goes from 8 catchments and 36m ha to 20 and 93m ha.
- **All four recruitment zones now carry a water instrument.** Fitzroy and Burdekin via Reef Credits, Lachlan and Murrumbidgee via ACWIS. Previously two of four.
- **The twelve ACWIS catchments are our assessment, not a scheme boundary.** If anyone asks "who says these twelve", the answer is us, on grazing dominance and sediment export. The brief says so on screen.
- **The carbon and water overlap is deliberately not shown.** Sixteen catchments carry both. It is a portfolio construction fact, useful to us for placing field agents, not to a buyer. Its home is the co-benefits capability and the property view.
- **Two fills only, and that was forced.** Olive carbon plus blue water passes the colour blindness check at 3.5 times the threshold. A third fill was not available: orange collides with the olive, violets collide with the blue, teal fails on tritan.
