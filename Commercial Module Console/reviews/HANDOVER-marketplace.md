## Three things you may want to decide rather than leave to Claude Design

I have posed all three in the brief, so they will get answered either way. A view from me on each in case you would rather set them.

**Does the obligation instrument go to market at all.** This is the only place the console asserts something about BHP's own compliance position, and it is the strongest claim anywhere in the product. Holding a biodiversity certificate contributes evidence toward a disclosure rather than answering it, so *closes 3 of 14* is both the line that sells and the line that overclaims. My view is that the instrument is worth building and the phrasing needs someone who owns that risk to sign it. Build the form now, settle the words before it is shown.

**Does the instrument replace the four credit cards or sit above them.** Replacing is cleaner and it loses the per-line detail a buyer eventually asks for: volume, term, issuing entity. My view is above rather than instead, with the cards becoming the detail behind the instrument.

**Live miniatures or fixed renders.** This decides whether the marketplace is a demo surface or a real one. Live is materially more convincing and it is five more surfaces to build. My view is that two live and three fixed is a worse outcome than five fixed, because the inconsistency is visible and it reads as unfinished.

## What will probably come back weak, so you know what to look at

Based on how the last few rounds returned.

**The miniatures may come back as generic charts.** The test is simple: cover the card title and see whether you can still tell which capability it is. If you cannot, it is decoration rather than a preview.

**A third area fill colour may appear on the credits instrument.** It does not need one. Solid, striped unfilled, and empty track is the whole vocabulary, and it is already in use on the map. If a new fill shows up, that is the thing to send back.

**The enable transition will probably be deferred**, because it is motion and motion is the easiest thing to leave until later. It is also the actual product moment, so it is worth asking for.

**The finding may get bigger and stay a sentence.** The point is not larger type. The point is that the picture becomes the argument and the sentence mostly goes away.

**`0 enabled` may come back reworded rather than removed.**
