## How to prompt this to get the most out of it

Six rules, all of them earned from things that went wrong in this build rather than from theory.

**One pass, one surface, one intent per prompt.** A system wide clean-up in a single message produces a redesign you did not ask for. Polish and build must never share a prompt, because the build gets the attention and the polish gets treated as trim.

**Deletions in their own prompt, first.** They cannot regress anything. Send them, look at the result, then send the two fixes. If something breaks you know which prompt did it.

**The exclusion sentence is the most valuable line you will write.** *Nothing else on the console changes.* That single sentence has done more work across these briefs than any instruction.

**Every item has to be checkable in thirty seconds.** "Remove the tilde from every number" is checkable. "Make it crisper" is not. If you cannot verify it at a glance under time pressure tonight, it is not a tonight item.

**Number the items and ask for the list back.** Ask it to report against your numbers and to say explicitly where it disagreed and left something alone. This is how you catch the silent skip, which is what happened to the "0 enabled" counter and to the instruction to leave the obligation claims provisional. Both were in a list and both were quietly dropped.

**For anything factual, give the reason and not just the instruction.** *Remove the Climate Active tag* invites it to swap in a different framework. *Remove the Climate Active tag because the programme is being closed, and do not replace it with another framework* does not.
