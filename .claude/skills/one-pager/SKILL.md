# One-Pager Skill

The hardest deliverable to do well. Compression is the work.

## Workflow

1. **Identify the audience and the decision.** A one-pager is always for someone, deciding something. Confirm both before drafting.
2. **Use** `templates/one-pager.md` as the skeleton.
3. **Draft long, then cut hard.** First pass: get every claim on the page. Second pass: cut to what fits on one screen. Third pass: reorder so the first sentence is the most important.
4. **Quality bar:**
   - **TL;DR** — one sentence answering "what should the reader do?"
   - **Why now** — the trigger / urgency
   - **What we know** — 3-5 evidence points, each with a number or citation
   - **What we propose** — the recommendation, made concrete
   - **Risks / open questions** — honest, ranked
   - **Ask** — the specific decision or resource you need
5. **Save to** `memory/deliverables/one-pagers/YYYY-MM-DD-<slug>.md`.
6. **Run through `critic`** before sharing externally.

## Heuristics

- If you can't fit it on one page, the thinking isn't done yet.
- Numbers > adjectives. Always.
- The reader should be able to make the decision from the page alone — no meeting required.
